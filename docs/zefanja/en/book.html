He Rejoices over You

The whole book of Zephaniah

The complete text of Zephaniah in the translation used in these devotionals.

Chapter 1

1The word of the LORD that came to Zephaniah son of Cushi, son of Gedaliah, son of Amariah, son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah.

2I will sweep away, sweep away everythingfrom the face of the ground –declares the LORD.3I will sweep away man and beast;I will sweep away the birds of the heavensand the fish of the sea,and the stumbling blocks with the wicked.I will cut off mankindfrom the face of the ground –declares the LORD.4I will stretch out my hand against Judahand against all the inhabitants of Jerusalem.I will cut off from this place the remnant of Baal,the name of the idol-priests together with the priests,5and those who bow down on the rooftopsto the host of the heavens,and those who bow down and swear to the LORDand swear by their king,6and those who turn back from following the LORD,who have not sought the LORDand have not inquired of him.7Silence before the Lord, the LORD!For the day of the LORD is near;for the LORD has prepared a sacrifice,he has consecrated those he invited.8On the day of the LORD's sacrificeI will punish the officials and the king's sonsand all who clothe themselves in foreign clothing.9And on that day I will punisheveryone who leaps over the threshold,those who fill the house of their masterwith violence and deceit.10On that day – declares the LORD –a cry will sound from the Fish Gate,wailing from the Second Quarter,and a great crash from the hills.11Wail, you inhabitants of the Mortar!For all the people of Canaan are destroyed;all who weigh out silver are cut off.12At that time I will search Jerusalem with lamps,and I will punish the menwho have thickened on their dregs,who say in their heart:"The LORD does no good,and he does no evil."13Their wealth will become plunderand their houses a desolation.They will build houses but not live in them;they will plant vineyards but not drink their wine.14Near is the great day of the LORD,near and hurrying very fast.Listen! The day of the LORD!Bitterly the warrior cries out there.15A day of rage is that day,a day of distress and anguish,a day of wreck and ruin,a day of darkness and deep gloom,a day of cloud and thick darkness,16a day of ram's horn and battle cryagainst the fortified citiesand against the lofty corner towers.17I will bring distress on mankind,and they will walk like the blind,for they have sinned against the LORD.Their blood will be poured out like dust,and their flesh like dung.18Neither their silver nor their goldwill be able to rescue themon the day of the rage of the LORD.In the fire of his jealousythe whole earth will be devoured;for a full end, yes, a terrifying endhe will make of all the inhabitants of the earth.

Chapter 2

1Gather yourselves together, yes, gather,O nation without longing,2before the decree is born –like chaff the day passes by –before there comes upon youthe burning anger of the LORD,before there comes upon youthe day of the anger of the LORD.3Seek the LORD, all you humble of the land,who have done his justice.Seek righteousness, seek humility;perhaps you will be hiddenon the day of the anger of the LORD.4For Gaza will be forsaken,and Ashkelon a desolation;Ashdod – at noon they will drive her out,and Ekron will be uprooted.5Woe to the inhabitants of the seacoast,the nation of the Cherethites!The word of the LORD is against you,Canaan, land of the Philistines:I will make you perishuntil there is no inhabitant.6The seacoast will become pastures,meadows for shepherdsand pens for flocks.7The coast will belongto the remnant of the house of Judah;there they will graze,in the houses of Ashkelonthey will lie down at evening.For the LORD their God will care for themand turn back their captivity.8I have heard the reproach of Moaband the revilings of the Ammonites,with which they reproached my peopleand made themselves great against their territory.9Therefore, as I live –declares the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel –Moab will become like Sodom,and the Ammonites like Gomorrah:a place possessed by nettles, and a salt pit,and a desolation for ever.The remnant of my people will plunder them,and the rest of my nation will inherit them.10This they will have for their pride,because they reproached and made themselves greatagainst the people of the LORD of hosts.11Fearsome will the LORD be against them,for he will make all the gods of the earth waste away;and they will bow down to him, each from its place,all the coastlands of the nations.12You too, Cushites –they are slain by my sword.13And he will stretch out his hand against the northand make Assyria perish,and he will make Nineveh a desolation,dry as the wilderness.14Herds will lie down in her midst,every beast of a nation;the desert owl and the hedgehogwill lodge among her capitals.A voice will sing in the window,devastation on the doorstep,for the cedar work is laid bare.15This is the exulting citythat sat secure,that said in her heart:"I am, and there is none besides me."How she has become a desolation,a lair for wild beasts!Everyone who passes by herhisses and shakes his fist.

Chapter 3

1Woe to the rebellious and defiled one,the oppressing city!2She has not listened to the voice;she has not accepted discipline.In the LORD she has not trusted;to her God she has not drawn near.3Her officials in her midstare roaring lions;her judges are wolves at eveningthat leave nothing for the morning.4Her prophets are reckless,men of treachery;her priests profane what is holy;they do violence to the instruction.5The LORD in her midst is righteous;he does no injustice.Morning by morning he brings his justice to light;it never fails.But the unjust knows no shame.6I have cut off nations;their corner towers are desolate.I have laid waste their streets,with no one passing through.Their cities lie in ruins,with no one left, with no inhabitant.7I said: "Surely you will fear me,you will accept discipline" –then her dwelling would not be cut off,all that I had appointed for her.But they rose earlyand made all their deeds corrupt.8Therefore wait for me – declares the LORD –for the day when I rise up for plunder.For my judgment is to gather nations,to assemble kingdoms,to pour out on them my fury,all my burning anger;for in the fire of my jealousythe whole earth will be devoured.9For then I will change the lip of the peoplesinto a pure lip,so that all of them call on the name of the LORDand serve him shoulder to shoulder.10From beyond the rivers of Cushmy worshippers, the daughter of my dispersed ones,will bring my offering.11On that day you will not be put to shamefor all your deedsby which you rebelled against me;for then I will remove from your midstthose who exult in your pride,and you will no longer be haughtyon my holy mountain.12I will leave in your midsta people poor and lowly,and they will shelter in the name of the LORD.13The remnant of Israelwill do no injustice and speak no lie,and no deceitful tonguewill be found in their mouth.For they will graze and lie down,and no one will make them afraid.14Sing for joy, Daughter Zion!Shout aloud, Israel!Be glad and exult with all your heart,Daughter Jerusalem!15The LORD has taken away the judgments against you;he has turned away your enemy.The King of Israel, the LORD, is in your midst;you will fear evil no more.16On that day it will be said to Jerusalem:"Do not fear, Zion!Do not let your hands hang limp!17The LORD your God is in your midst,a warrior who saves.He beams with gladness over you;he is silent in his love;he rejoices over you with a song of joy."18Those who grieve far from the appointed feast I have gathered;they were from you –a burden on her, a reproach.19Look, at that time I will dealwith all who afflict you.I will save the lameand assemble the outcast,and I will make them a praise and a namein all the earth where they were put to shame.20At that time I will bring you home,at the time when I assemble you;for I will make you a name and a praiseamong all the peoples of the earth,when I turn back your captivitybefore your eyes, says the LORD.
