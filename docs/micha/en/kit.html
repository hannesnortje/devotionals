Who Is a God Like You?

Small-group kit

Seven sessions for home groups and small groups – one per chapter, 75 to 90 minutes each.

Leader's guide as PDF (A4) All slides as PDF

  1. Session 1 · Micah 1:1–16

    The LORD Comes Down

    When the holy God comes down, every false “high place” we trust in melts like wax – and the prophet who sees it coming does not gloat, but weeps for his own people.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  2. Session 2 · Micah 2:1–13

    Greed and the Breaker

    God sees the schemes we devise at night and the power we use against the weak, he will not be silenced by soothing preachers – and yet he himself goes ahead of his people as the one who breaks open the way.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  3. Session 3 · Micah 3:1–12

    Filled with Power

    God holds leaders, judges and preachers to account for how they treat his people, and he will not be bribed by pious slogans – but he fills those who rely on his Spirit with power, justice and might to speak the truth.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  4. Session 4 · Micah 4:1–14

    Swords into Ploughshares

    Judgement is not God's last word: the mountain left in ruins becomes the mountain the nations stream to, and on the hard road through exile the LORD gathers the lame, turns his people's pain into birth pains and rules even over the plans of their enemies.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  5. Session 5 · Micah 5:1–14

    The Ruler from Bethlehem

    Out of little Bethlehem God brings a Ruler whose origins reach back to ancient days, who stands and shepherds in the strength of the LORD and is himself our peace – and under him his people become dew and lion among the nations, while he cuts away every false support they lean on.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  6. Session 6 · Micah 6:1–16

    What the LORD Seeks

    The LORD who redeemed his people cannot be bought with offerings; he has told us what is good – to do justice, to love faithful love and to walk humbly with our God – and he looks for it in the market, at the workbench and in our weighing of others.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  7. Session 7 · Micah 7:1–20

    Who Is a God Like You?

    When every human support collapses, the one who watches for the LORD finds that he hears, pleads our case and brings us into the light – for no one is like him: he bears away guilt, delights in faithful love and casts our sins into the depths of the sea.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶
