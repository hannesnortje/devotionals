Who Is a God Like You?

The whole book of Micah

The complete text of Micah in the translation used in these devotionals.

Chapter 1

1The word of the LORD that came to Micah of Moresheth in the days of Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, which he saw concerning Samaria and Jerusalem.

2Hear, you peoples, all of you!Listen, O earth, and all that fills it!Let the Lord, the LORD, be a witness against you,the Lord from his holy temple.3For look: the LORD is coming out of his place;he comes down and treads on the high places of the earth.4The mountains melt beneath him,and the valleys split open,like wax before the fire,like water poured down a slope.5All this is for the rebellion of Jacoband for the sins of the house of Israel.What is the rebellion of Jacob?Is it not Samaria?And what are the high places of Judah?Is it not Jerusalem?6So I will make Samaria a heap of rubble in the open field,a place for planting vineyards;I will pour her stones down into the valleyand lay bare her foundations.7All her carved images will be smashed,all her wages will be burned with fire,and all her idols I will make a desolation;for from the wages of a prostitute she gathered them,and to the wages of a prostitute they will return.8Because of this I will lament and wail;I will go stripped and naked;I will make a lament like the jackalsand a mourning like the ostriches.9For her wounds are incurable;it has come as far as Judah,it has reached the gate of my people,as far as Jerusalem.10In Gath do not tell it;do not weep at all.In Beth-leaphrahroll yourself in the dust.11Pass on your way,inhabitant of Shaphir, in nakedness and shame.The inhabitant of Zaananhas not come out.The lament of Beth-ezel –it will take from you its standing place.12For the inhabitant of Marothwaits in anguish for good,for evil has come down from the LORDto the gate of Jerusalem.13Harness the chariot to the steed,inhabitant of Lachish!She was the beginning of sin for Daughter Zion,for in you were found the rebellions of Israel.14Therefore you will give parting giftsto Moresheth-gath;the houses of Achzib will be a deceitto the kings of Israel.15Once more I will bring the one who takes possession to you,inhabitant of Mareshah;as far as Adullamthe glory of Israel will come.16Make yourself bald and shave your headfor the children of your delight;make your baldness as wide as the vulture's,for they have gone from you into exile.

Chapter 2

1Woe to those who devise wickednessand work evil on their beds!In the light of morning they carry it out,because it is in the power of their hand.2They covet fields and seize them,and houses, and take them away;they oppress a man and his house,a person and his inheritance.3Therefore thus says the LORD:Look, against this family I am devising evil,from which you cannot remove your necks;and you will not walk with heads held high,for it will be a time of evil.4On that day they will take up a taunt-song against youand wail a bitter lament:"It is done!" one says."We are utterly ruined!He exchanges the portion of my people.How he takes it away from me!To the renegade he divides up our fields."5Therefore you will have no oneto cast the measuring line by lotin the assembly of the LORD.6"Do not preach!" – so they preach."One should not preach about such things;disgrace will not overtake us."7Should this be said, house of Jacob:"Is the Spirit of the LORD impatient?Are these his deeds?"Do my words not do goodto the one who walks uprightly?8But lately my people has risen up as an enemy.From off the garment you strip the cloakfrom those who pass by in trust,like men coming home from war.9The women of my people you drive out,each from the house she delights in;from her little children you take awaymy splendour for ever.10Get up and go!For this is not the resting place;because of uncleanness it brings destruction,a grievous destruction.11If a man came walking in wind and falsehoodand lied: "I will preach to you about wine and strong drink!" –he would be the preacher for this people.12I will surely gather all of you, Jacob;I will surely assemble the remnant of Israel.I will put them together like sheep in a fold,like a flock in the midst of its pasture;they will be noisy with people.13The one who breaks open goes up before them;they break through, pass through the gateand go out by it.Their king passes on before them,and the LORD at their head.

Chapter 3

1And I said:Hear, please, you heads of Jacoband rulers of the house of Israel!Is it not for you to know justice?2You who hate good and love evil,who tear their skin from off themand their flesh from off their bones;3who eat the flesh of my peopleand strip their skin from off them,break their bones in piecesand chop them up like meat for the pot,like flesh in the cauldron.4Then they will cry out to the LORD,but he will not answer them;he will hide his face from them at that time,because they have made their deeds evil.5Thus says the LORD concerning the prophetswho lead my people astray,who, when they have something to bite with their teeth,call out "Peace!",but whoever puts nothing into their mouths,against him they sanctify war:6Therefore it will be night for you, without vision,and darkness for you, without divination.The sun will go down on the prophets,and the day will grow dark over them.7The seers will be ashamedand the diviners disgraced;they will all cover their lips,for there is no answer from God.8But as for me, I am filled with power,with the Spirit of the LORD,with justice and with might,to declare to Jacob his rebellionand to Israel his sin.9Hear this, please, you heads of the house of Jacoband rulers of the house of Israel,who abhor justiceand twist everything that is straight,10who build Zion with bloodand Jerusalem with injustice!11Her heads judge for a bribe,her priests teach for a price,and her prophets divine for money;yet they lean on the LORD and say:"Is not the LORD in our midst?No evil will come upon us."12Therefore, because of you,Zion will be ploughed as a field,Jerusalem will become heaps of ruins,and the mountain of the house a wooded height.

Chapter 4

1And it will come to pass in the last daysthat the mountain of the house of the LORDwill stand firm as the highest of the mountainsand be lifted up above the hills,and peoples will stream to it.2Many nations will come and say:"Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD,to the house of the God of Jacob,so that he may teach us his waysand we may walk in his paths."For from Zion instruction will go out,and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.3He will judge between many peoplesand settle disputes for mighty nations far away.They will beat their swords into ploughsharesand their spears into pruning knives.Nation will not lift up sword against nation,and they will no longer learn war.4They will sit, each under his vineand under his fig tree,and no one will make them afraid;for the mouth of the LORD of hosts has spoken.5For all the peoples walk,each in the name of its god,but we will walk in the name of the LORD our Godfor ever and ever.6On that day – declares the LORD –I will gather the lameand assemble the outcast,and the one on whom I brought evil.7I will make the lame a remnant,and the one driven far away a mighty nation;and the LORD will reign over them on Mount Zionfrom now on and for ever.8And you, tower of the flock,hill of Daughter Zion,to you it will come:the former dominion will come,the kingdom of Daughter Jerusalem.9Now, why do you cry out so loudly?Is there no king in you?Has your counsellor perished,that pain has seized you like a woman giving birth?10Writhe and push, Daughter Zion,like a woman giving birth!For now you will go out of the cityand live in the open field;you will come as far as Babylon.There you will be rescued;there the LORD will redeem youfrom the hand of your enemies.11And now many nationshave gathered against you,who say: "Let her be defiled,and let our eyes gloat over Zion!"12But they do not know the plans of the LORD,and they do not understand his counsel:that he has gathered them like sheaves to the threshing floor.13Get up and thresh, Daughter Zion!For I will make your horn iron,and your hoofs I will make bronze;you will crush many peoples.And I will devote their gain to the LORD,their wealth to the Lord of all the earth.14Now gather your troops, daughter of troops!A siege has been laid against us;with the staff they strike the judge of Israelon the cheek. (English Bibles: 5:1)

Chapter 5

1But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah,too small to be among the clans of Judah,from you one will come out for meto be ruler in Israel;his origins are from of old,from ancient days. (English Bibles: 5:2; from here on add one to the verse number)2Therefore he will give them upuntil the time when she who is in labour has given birth;then the rest of his brothers will returnto the children of Israel.3He will stand and shepherdin the strength of the LORD,in the majesty of the name of the LORD his God.And they will dwell secure,for now he will be greatto the ends of the earth.4And this one will be peace.When Assyria comes into our landand when he treads in our fortresses,we will raise against him seven shepherdsand eight princes of men.5They will shepherd the land of Assyria with the sword,and the land of Nimrod at its gates.And he will rescue us from Assyriawhen he comes into our landand when he treads within our borders.6And the remnant of Jacob will bein the midst of many peopleslike dew from the LORD,like showers on the grass,which does not wait for a mannor hope for the children of men.7And the remnant of Jacob will be among the nations,in the midst of many peoples,like a lion among the animals of the forest,like a young lion among the flocks of sheep,which, if it passes through, tramples and tears,and there is no one to rescue.8Your hand will be lifted high over your adversaries,and all your enemies will be cut off.9And it will come to pass on that day – declares the LORD –that I will cut off your horses from among youand make your chariots perish.10I will cut off the cities of your landand tear down all your fortresses.11I will cut off sorceries from your hand,and you will have no more fortune-tellers.12I will cut off your carved imagesand your sacred pillars from among you,and you will no longer bow downto the work of your hands.13I will uproot your Asherah poles from among youand destroy your cities.14And in anger and in wrath I will execute vengeanceon the nations that did not listen.

Chapter 6

1Hear, please, what the LORD says:Get up, plead the case before the mountains,and let the hills hear your voice!2Hear, you mountains, the case of the LORD,you enduring ones, foundations of the earth!For the LORD has a case against his people,and with Israel he will argue it.3My people, what have I done to you?How have I wearied you? Answer me!4For I brought you up from the land of Egypt,and from the house of slaves I redeemed you;and I sent before youMoses, Aaron and Miriam.5My people, rememberwhat Balak king of Moab planned,and what Balaam son of Beor answered him –from Shittim to Gilgal –so that you may know the righteous acts of the LORD.6With what shall I come before the LORD,and bow down before God on high?Shall I come before him with burnt offerings,with calves a year old?7Will the LORD be pleased with thousands of rams,with ten thousand rivers of oil?Shall I give my firstborn for my rebellion,the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?8He has told you, O man, what is good,and what the LORD seeks from you:only to do justice,to love faithful love,and to walk humbly with your God.9The voice of the LORD calls out to the city –and it is wisdom to see your name:Hear the rod, and the one who appointed it!10Are there still in the house of the wickedtreasures of wickedness,and the short measure that is cursed?11Can I count anyone pure with wicked scalesand with a bag of deceitful weights?12Her rich men are full of violence,her inhabitants speak falsehood,and their tongue is deceit in their mouth.13So I too have begun to strike you,to make you desolate because of your sins.14You will eat, but not be satisfied,and the hunger will be inside you;you will put things aside, but not save them,and what you save I will give to the sword.15You will sow, but not reap;you will tread olives, but not anoint yourself with oil,and grapes for new wine, but not drink wine.16The statutes of Omri are kept,and all the practices of the house of Ahab,and you walk in their counsels;so I will make you a desolation,and her inhabitants something to hiss at;and you will bear the reproach of my people.

Chapter 7

1Woe is me! For I have becomelike one gathering summer fruit,like one gleaning after the grape harvest:there is no cluster to eat,no early fig that my soul longs for.2The faithful one has perished from the land,and there is no one upright among people.They all lie in wait for blood;each hunts his brother with a net.3Their hands are on evil, to do it well:the official asks, and the judge – for a payment;and the great man speaks out the craving of his soul;and so they weave it together.4The best of them is like a briar,the most upright worse than a thorn hedge.The day of your watchmen, your punishment, has come;now their confusion will be.5Do not trust a friend,do not rely on a close companion;from her who lies in your embraceguard the doors of your mouth.6For son treats father with contempt,daughter rises up against her mother,daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law;a man's enemies are the people of his own house.7But as for me, I will watch for the LORD;I will wait for the God of my salvation;my God will hear me.8Do not rejoice over me, my enemy!When I have fallen, I rise;when I sit in darkness,the LORD is light for me.9The rage of the LORD I will bear,for I have sinned against him,until he pleads my caseand brings about justice for me.He will bring me out into the light;I will see his righteousness.10And my enemy will see it,and shame will cover herwho said to me: "Where is he, the LORD your God?"My eyes will look on her;now she will be trampled downlike mud in the streets.11A day for building your walls!On that day the boundary will be pushed far out.12On that day they will come to you,from Assyria and the cities of Egypt,and from Egypt to the River,from sea to sea and from mountain to mountain.13But the earth will become a desolationbecause of those who live in it,as the fruit of their deeds.14Shepherd your people with your staff,the flock of your inheritance,who live alone in a forest,in the midst of a fruitful land;let them graze in Bashan and Gileadas in ancient days.15As in the days when you came out of the land of Egypt,I will show him wonders.16Nations will see and be ashamedof all their might;they will lay a hand on their mouth;their ears will be deaf.17They will lick dust like the snake,like the creatures that crawl on the earth;they will come trembling out of their strongholds;to the LORD our God they will come in dread,and they will fear you.18Who is a God like you,who bears away guiltand passes over rebellionfor the remnant of his inheritance?He does not hold on to his anger for ever,for he delights in faithful love.19He will again have compassion on us;he will tread our guilt underfoot.And you will cast all their sinsinto the depths of the sea.20You will give truth to Jacob,faithful love to Abraham,as you swore to our fathersfrom days of old.
