Yet I Will Rejoice

Small-group kit

Three sessions for home groups and small groups – one per chapter, 75 to 90 minutes each.

Leader's guide as PDF (A4) All slides as PDF

  1. Session 1 · Habakkuk 1:1–17

    How Long, LORD?

    God welcomes our honest lament: he is never absent when heaven feels silent, he rules even over the powers that frighten us, and when his answer confuses us we can hold on to who he is – our Rock from of old.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  2. Session 2 · Habakkuk 2:1–20

    Write Down the Vision

    While we wait at the guard post, God's promise is sure: the proud will not stand, but the righteous will live by his faithfulness – trusting God until the appointed time, when the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  3. Session 3 · Habakkuk 3:1–19

    Yet I Will Rejoice

    Remembering what God has done gives hope for what lies ahead: even when every earthly security falls away and our bodies tremble, we can rejoice in the God of our salvation, because our joy rests on who he is, not on what he gives.
