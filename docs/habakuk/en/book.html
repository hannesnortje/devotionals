Yet I Will Rejoice

The whole book of Habakkuk

The complete text of Habakkuk in the translation used in these devotionals.

Chapter 1

1The oracle that Habakkuk the prophet saw.

2How long, LORD, shall I cry for help,and you do not hear?I cry out to you, "Violence!"and you do not save.3Why do you make me see wickedness,and why do you look on misery?Destruction and violence are before me;there is strife, and contention rises up.4Therefore instruction grows numb,and justice never comes forth.For the wicked surrounds the righteous;therefore justice comes forth twisted.5See among the nations, and look;be astonished – be astounded!For I am doing a work in your daysthat you would not believe if it were told.6For look, I am raising up the Chaldeans,that bitter and impetuous nation,who march across the breadth of the earthto take possession of dwellings that are not their own.7Terrible and dreadful is he;from himself come forth his justice and his dignity.8His horses are swifter than leopardsand fiercer than wolves at evening.His horsemen gallop on,his horsemen come from far away;they fly like a vulture that hurries to devour.9All of them come for violence;the set of their faces is forward,and he gathers captives like sand.10He mocks at kings,and rulers are a joke to him.He laughs at every fortress;he heaps up dust and captures it.11Then he sweeps on like the wind and passes by,and he becomes guilty –he whose strength is his god.12Are you not from of old, LORD,my God, my Holy One?We will not die.LORD, you have appointed him for judgment;O Rock, you have established him to reprove.13Your eyes are too pure to see evil,and you cannot look on misery.Why do you look on the treacherousand keep silent when the wicked swallows upone more righteous than he?14You make people like the fish of the sea,like crawling things that have no ruler.15He brings them all up with a hook,he drags them away in his netand gathers them in his dragnet;so he is glad and rejoices.16Therefore he sacrifices to his netand burns incense to his dragnet,for through them his portion is fatand his food is rich.17Is he then to keep emptying his netand go on killing nations without sparing?

Chapter 2

1I will stand at my guard postand take my place on the rampart;I will watch to see what he will say to meand what I shall answer concerning my reproof.

2Then the LORD answered me and said:

Write down the visionand make it plain on the tablets,so that whoever reads it may run.3For the vision is still for the appointed time;it pants toward the end, and it will not deceive.If it lingers, wait for it,for it will surely come; it will not delay.4Look, he is puffed up – his soul is not upright within him;but the righteous will live by his faithfulness.5And moreover, wine is treacherous;an arrogant man does not stay at home.He opens his throat wide like the grave;he is like death and is never sated.He gathers to himself all the nationsand assembles to himself all the peoples.6Will not all of these take up a taunt against him,a mocking song, riddles about him, and say:Woe to the one who heaps up what is not his own –how long? –and loads himself heavy with pledges!7Will not those who bite you rise up suddenly,and those who make you tremble wake up,and you will become plunder for them?8Because you have plundered many nations,all who are left of the peoples will plunder you,because of human blood and violence to the land,to the city and all who live in it.9Woe to the one who gains evil gain for his house,to set his nest on high,to be snatched from the grip of evil!10You have counselled shame for your houseby cutting off many peoples,and you are sinning against your own life.11For a stone from the wall will cry out,and a rafter from the woodwork will answer it.12Woe to the one who builds a town with bloodand founds a city on injustice!13Look, is it not from the LORD of hoststhat peoples toil only for the fireand nations weary themselves for nothing?14For the earth will be filledwith the knowledge of the glory of the LORD,as the waters cover the sea.15Woe to the one who makes his neighbour drink,mixing in your wrath and making him drunk as well,in order to look on their nakedness!16You are sated with disgrace instead of glory.Drink, you too, and show your foreskin!The cup in the LORD's right hand will come around to you,and utter disgrace will come upon your glory.17For the violence done to Lebanon will cover you,and the destruction of the beasts that terrified them,because of human blood and violence to the land,to the city and all who live in it.18What use is a carved image,that its maker has carved it,a cast image and a teacher of lies?For its maker trusts in what he himself has formedwhen he makes mute idols.19Woe to the one who says to wood, "Wake up!""Awake!" to a silent stone.Can it teach?Look, it is overlaid with gold and silver,and there is no breath at all inside it.20But the LORD is in his holy temple –silence before him, all the earth!

Chapter 3

1A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, according to shigionot.

2LORD, I have heard the report of you;I fear, LORD, your work.In the midst of the years, bring it to life;in the midst of the years, make it known.In turmoil, remember compassion.3God came from Teman,the Holy One from Mount Paran.SelaHis splendour covered the heavens,and the earth was full of his praise.4His brightness was like the light;rays broke from his hand,and there his power was veiled.5Before him went pestilence,and plague came forth at his feet.6He stood and measured the earth;he looked and made the nations start.The everlasting mountains were shattered,the ancient hills sank low;the ancient ways are his.7I saw the tents of Cushan under calamity;the tent curtains of the land of Midian trembled.8Was it against the rivers that the LORD burned?Was your anger against the rivers,or your rage against the sea,when you rode on your horses,your chariots of salvation?9Your bow was laid bare, utterly bare;sworn were the rods – a word.SelaYou split the earth into rivers.10The mountains saw you and writhed;a torrent of water passed over.The deep gave its voice;it lifted its hands on high.11Sun and moon stood still in their dwellingat the light of your arrows as they sped,at the brightness of the flash of your spear.12In fury you marched through the earth;in anger you threshed the nations.13You went out for the salvation of your people,for the salvation of your anointed.You crushed the head of the house of the wicked,laying bare the foundation up to the neck.Sela14With his own rods you pierced the head of his warriors,who stormed out to scatter me;their exulting was as if to devourthe poor in a hiding place.15You trod the sea with your horses,the surging of many waters.16I heard, and my insides trembled;at the sound my lips quivered.Rot entered my bones,and where I stand I tremble.Yet I will wait quietly for the day of distressto come up against the people who invade us.17Though the fig tree does not blossomand there is no fruit on the vines,though the olive crop failsand the terraced fields yield no food,though the flock is cut off from the penand there are no cattle in the stalls –18yet I will exult in the LORD,I will rejoice in the God of my salvation.19The LORD, the Lord, is my strength;he makes my feet like those of the deerand lets me tread on my high places.For the choir director, on my stringed instruments.
