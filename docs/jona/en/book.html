Journey through Jonah

The whole book of Jonah

The complete text of Jonah in the translation used in these devotionals. Verse numbers follow the Hebrew: English Bibles number 2:1 as 1:17 and 2:2–11 as 2:1–10.

Chapter 1

1The word of the LORD came to Jonah son of Amittai: 2"Get up, go to Nineveh, the great city, and cry out against it, for their evil has come up before my face." 3So Jonah got up – to flee to Tarshish, away from the face of the LORD. He went down to Joppa and found a ship bound for Tarshish. He paid the fare and went down into it, to go with them to Tarshish, away from the face of the LORD.

4But the LORD hurled a great wind onto the sea, and there was a great storm on the sea, so that the ship threatened to break apart. 5The sailors were afraid, and each cried out to his own god. They hurled the cargo that was in the ship into the sea to lighten it for themselves. But Jonah had gone down into the innermost part of the ship, had lain down, and had fallen into a deep sleep. 6The captain came to him and said to him, "What are you doing asleep? Get up, call out to your god! Perhaps that god will give a thought to us, and we will not perish." 7Then they said to one another, "Come, let us cast lots, so that we may know on whose account this evil has come upon us." So they cast lots, and the lot fell on Jonah. 8Then they said to him, "Tell us, please, on whose account this evil has come upon us! What is your work, and where do you come from? What is your country, and from what people are you?" 9He said to them, "A Hebrew am I, and the LORD, the God of heaven, I fear – he who made the sea and the dry land." 10Then the men feared with a great fear and said to him, "What is this you have done!" For the men knew that he was fleeing from the face of the LORD, because he had told them. 11And they said to him, "What shall we do to you, so that the sea will calm down for us?" For the sea was growing more and more stormy. 12He said to them, "Pick me up and hurl me into the sea, and the sea will calm down for you. For I know that it is on my account that this great storm has come upon you." 13The men rowed hard to get back to dry land, but they could not, for the sea was growing more and more stormy against them. 14Then they called out to the LORD and said, "Please, LORD, do not let us perish for this man's life, and do not lay innocent blood on us! For you, LORD, have done as you pleased." 15Then they picked Jonah up and hurled him into the sea, and the sea stopped its raging. 16Then the men feared the LORD with a great fear; they offered a sacrifice to the LORD and made vows.

Chapter 2

1But the LORD appointed a great fish to swallow Jonah. And Jonah was in the belly of the fish three days and three nights. (English Bibles: 1:17) 2Then Jonah prayed to the LORD his God from the belly of the fish 3and said:

I called out of my distress to the LORD,and he answered me;from the belly of the grave I cried for help –you heard my voice.4You had thrown me into the deep,into the heart of the seas,and the current surrounded me;all your breakers and your wavespassed over me.5And I – I said, "I am driven awayfrom before your eyes.Yet I will look againtowards your holy temple."6Waters closed around me up to my throat,the deep surrounded me,seaweed was wrapped around my head.7To the roots of the mountains I went down;the earth – its bars closed behind me for ever.But you brought my life up from the pit,LORD my God.8When my soul was fainting within me,I remembered the LORD,and my prayer came to you,into your holy temple.9Those who cling to worthless idolsabandon their faithful love.10But I, with a voice of thanksgiving,will sacrifice to you;what I have vowed I will pay.Salvation belongs to the LORD!

11Then the LORD spoke to the fish, and it vomited Jonah out onto the dry land.

Chapter 3

1The word of the LORD came to Jonah a second time: 2"Get up, go to Nineveh, the great city, and cry out to it the message that I am telling you." 3So Jonah got up and went to Nineveh, as the LORD had said. Now Nineveh was a great city – great even before God – a three days' journey across. 4Jonah began to go into the city, one day's journey, and he cried out and said, "Forty more days, and Nineveh will be overturned!" 5And the people of Nineveh believed God. They called a fast and put on sackcloth, from the greatest of them to the least. 6When the word reached the king of Nineveh, he got up from his throne, took off his royal robe, covered himself with sackcloth, and sat in the ashes. 7And he had it cried out in Nineveh and said: "By decree of the king and his great men: let neither man nor beast, herd nor flock taste anything; let them not graze, and let them not drink water. 8Let man and beast be covered with sackcloth, and let them call out to God with all their strength. Let each one turn from his evil way and from the violence that is in his hands. 9Who knows? God may turn and relent; he may turn from his burning anger, so that we do not perish." 10And God saw what they did, that they turned from their evil way. And God relented of the evil that he had said he would do to them, and he did not do it.

Chapter 4

1But to Jonah this was evil – a great evil – and his anger burned. 2He prayed to the LORD and said, "Please, LORD, is this not what I said while I was still in my own country? That is why I fled to Tarshish in the first place. For I knew that you are a gracious and compassionate God, slow to anger and abounding in faithful love, and one who relents over evil. 3So now, LORD, please take my life from me, for my death is better than my life." 4But the LORD said, "Is it right for your anger to burn?" 5Jonah went out of the city and sat down east of the city. There he made himself a shelter and sat under it in the shade, until he could see what would happen in the city. 6Then the LORD God appointed a leafy plant and made it grow up over Jonah, to be shade over his head, to deliver him from his evil. And Jonah rejoiced over the plant with great joy. 7But God appointed a worm when dawn came up the next day, and it struck the plant, so that it withered. 8When the sun rose, God appointed a scorching east wind, and the sun struck Jonah's head, so that he grew faint. He asked that he might die, and said, "My death is better than my life." 9But God said to Jonah, "Is it right for your anger to burn over the plant?" He said, "It is right for my anger to burn – to the point of death!" 10Then the LORD said, "You had pity on the plant, for which you did not labour and which you did not make grow; it came into being in a night and perished in a night. 11And should I not have pity on Nineveh, the great city, in which there are more than a hundred and twenty thousand people who do not know their right hand from their left – and many animals as well?"
