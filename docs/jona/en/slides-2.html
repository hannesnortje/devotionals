Journey through Jonah · Session 2

From the Depths

Jonah 2:1–11

To get started

Think of a time when you were completely stuck – a flat tyre, a lost key, a cancelled flight. What went through your mind?

Where we are in the story

Jonah has been thrown into the sea, and the storm has stopped. But the story is not over: the LORD appoints a great fish. From inside it, in total darkness, Jonah prays. Note: this chapter follows the Hebrew verse numbers – English Bibles number 2:1 as 1:17 and 2:2–11 as 2:1–10.

A look at the Hebrew

מָנָה

manah · “appoint”

God appoints the great fish (2:1) – and later the plant, the worm and the wind (4:6–8). The fish is not an accident or a punishment but God's chosen means of rescue.

A look at the Hebrew

חֶסֶד

chesed · “faithful love”

God's loyal covenant love that never gives up. “Those who cling to worthless idols abandon their faithful love” (2:9): whatever we cling to instead of God robs us of it.

A look at the Hebrew

יְשׁוּעָה

yeshu'ah · “salvation”

“Salvation belongs to the LORD!” (2:10). The name Yeshua – Jesus – comes from the same root.

The big idea

At the lowest point of his flight, Jonah discovers that God was in charge all along – and that salvation belongs to the LORD alone.

Discussion · 1/7

How do you read the great fish: as punishment or as rescue? What does the word “appointed” suggest?

Jonah 2:1

Discussion · 2/7

Jonah says to God, “You had thrown me into the deep” – although the sailors threw him. What does it mean to see God's hand behind our crisis?

Jonah 2:4

Discussion · 3/7

“I am driven away from before your eyes. Yet I will look again towards your holy temple.” What is the “yet” of faith, and where have you needed it?

Jonah 2:5

Discussion · 4/7

“To the roots of the mountains I went down … But you brought my life up from the pit.” How does this verse complete the “going down” we saw in chapter 1?

Jonah 2:7

Discussion · 5/7

Jonah says, “I remembered the LORD” when his soul was fainting. What helps you to remember God when you are exhausted?

Jonah 2:8

Discussion · 6/7

Jonah praises God before he is out of the fish. Why is praise in the dark so powerful – and so hard?

Jonah 2:10

Discussion · 7/7

Jesus calls his death and resurrection “the sign of Jonah” (Matthew 12:40). Where do you see the gospel in this chapter?

For the week ahead

  • Learn one verse of Jonah's psalm by heart this week, so that you carry God's word with you into “dark” days.
  • Name one “worthless idol” you cling to for security, and each day say aloud instead: “Salvation belongs to the LORD!”

Pray together

Read 2:3 together aloud. Then let each person who wishes name one situation where they need a “yet” of faith, and pray for each other briefly. Close by saying together: “Salvation belongs to the LORD!”
