Journey through Jonah

Small-group kit

Four sessions for home groups and small groups – one per chapter, 75 to 90 minutes each. Each session builds on that week's devotionals.

Leader's guide as PDF (A4) All slides as PDF

  1. Session 1 · Jonah 1:1–16

    On the Run

    God's call does not let go of us when we run – he sends a storm of grace that brings us, and those around us, back to him.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  2. Session 2 · Jonah 2:1–11

    From the Depths

    At the lowest point of his flight, Jonah discovers that God was in charge all along – and that salvation belongs to the LORD alone.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  3. Session 3 · Jonah 3:1–10

    Overturned

    God speaks to his runaway prophet a second time, and a violent city believes God and turns – God's grace overturns hearts, not only cities.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  4. Session 4 · Jonah 4:1–11

    The Unanswered Question

    Jonah is angry that God is gracious. Through a plant, a worm and a scorching wind God exposes his small heart – and asks a question the book leaves open for us to answer: should I not have pity?
