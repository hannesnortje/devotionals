Behind the Curtain

Small-group kit

Eleven sessions for home groups and small groups – one to three chapters each, 75 to 90 minutes each.

Leader's guide as PDF (A4) All slides as PDF

  1. Session 1 · Revelation 1:1–20

    The King among the Lampstands

    Revelation pulls back the curtain for small, pressured churches: the one who loves us and has freed us by his blood is the ruler of the kings of the earth, he walks among his churches, holds them in his right hand and says to them – and to us – “Do not be afraid.”

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  2. Session 10 · Revelation 17:1 – 19:21

    Come Out of Her

    Babylon – the seductive power of every age that rides on the beast, lives in luxury, trades in bodies and souls and is drunk with the blood of the saints – looks impressive but falls in a single hour; God calls his people to come out of her, not out of the world, and invites them to the wedding of the Lamb, whose Rider, Faithful and True, conquers by the word of his mouth.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  3. Session 11 · Revelation 20:1 – 22:21

    Come, Lord Jesus

    Behind the curtain Christ reigns now and Satan is on a chain, so that he cannot stop the gospel; the story that began in Eden ends not with an escape from the world but with God making all things new and dwelling with his people face to face – and so the church waits, awake and faithful, praying: “Amen. Come, Lord Jesus!”

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  4. Session 2 · Revelation 2:1–29

    Four Churches, One Lord

    The one who walks among the lampstands knows each church as it really is – Ephesus's loveless orthodoxy, Smyrna's rich poverty, Pergamum's quiet compromise and Thyatira's loving tolerance – and calls each of them, and us, to hear, repent, hold fast and conquer.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  5. Session 3 · Revelation 3:1–22

    Wake Up, Hold Fast, Open the Door

    Christ sees past reputation, size and wealth: he wakes up a church that only looks alive, opens a door for a church with little power, and knocks at the door of a church that thinks it needs nothing – and to all three he offers a place with him on his throne.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  6. Session 4 · Revelation 4:1 – 5:14

    Who Is Worthy?

    Behind the visible power of Rome John is shown the throne in heaven, where the Creator is worshipped, and in the middle of the throne a Lamb standing, as though slaughtered: God rules history, and his power looks like the cross.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  7. Session 5 · Revelation 6:1 – 7:17

    Who Can Stand?

    The Lamb who was slaughtered holds the seals: war, hunger and death ride out only when he opens them and only as far as God allows – and to the great question “who can stand?” the answer is a countless crowd from every nation, sealed by God and washed in the blood of the Lamb.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  8. Session 6 · Revelation 8:1 – 9:21

    Heaven Is Listening

    Heaven falls silent to hear the prayers of the saints, and those prayers come back to earth as God's action; the trumpets, like the plagues of Egypt, are limited warnings that call the world to repent – but judgement alone does not change the heart, and what the plagues cannot do, the Lamb does through his witnesses.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  9. Session 7 · Revelation 10:1 – 11:19

    Sweet and Bitter

    God's answer to a world that will not repent is his word in the mouth of his witnesses: the church eats the word, sweet and bitter, is measured and kept by God, bears witness in sackcloth, shares its Lord's death and resurrection – and the kingdom of the world has already become our Lord's and his Christ's.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  10. Session 8 · Revelation 12:1 – 13:18

    Whose Are You?

    Behind the curtain the dragon has already been thrown down by the blood of the Lamb; in his fury he works through beasts that imitate the Lamb and demand worship – but God's people, sealed before the beast ever appeared, conquer by the blood of the Lamb, the word of their testimony and endurance, for the question is not “when?” but “whose are you?”.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  11. Session 9 · Revelation 14:1 – 16:21

    The Song on the Shore

    After the dragon and the beasts, the curtain opens on the Lamb standing on Zion with his people, who bear his name and sing the song of Moses and of the Lamb before a single bowl is poured out – God's judgement on evil is real and just, but those who belong to the Lamb are kept, and the end of evil has already been decided.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶
