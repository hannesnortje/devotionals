Faithful in God's Household

Small-group kit

Six sessions for home groups and small groups – one per chapter, 75 to 90 minutes each. Each session builds on that week's devotionals.

Leader's guide as PDF (A4) All slides as PDF

  1. Session 1 · 1 Timothy 1:1–20

    Guard the Gospel

    Sound teaching is known by its fruit – love, a good conscience and sincere faith – and at its heart stands the gospel of grace for sinners like Paul and like us.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  2. Session 2 · 1 Timothy 2:1–15

    Prayer for All, Worship in Order

    God “wants all people to be saved”, so the church's first task is prayer for all – and its worship should reflect reconciled hearts, holy lives and God's good order.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  3. Session 3 · 1 Timothy 3:1–16

    Leaders for God's Household

    The church is God's household and “a pillar and foundation of the truth”, so its leaders are chosen for tested character more than talent – and all godliness flows from the mystery of Christ.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  4. Session 4 · 1 Timothy 4:1–16

    Training for Godliness

    False piety says “Don't touch” and makes people anxious; true godliness receives God's good gifts with thanksgiving and is trained day by day – in the leader's own life first.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  5. Session 5 · 1 Timothy 5:1–25

    The Church as a Family

    God's household is a family: we treat each other as fathers, mothers, brothers and sisters, care concretely for those who are truly alone, and honour and correct leaders fairly and without favouritism.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  6. Session 6 · 1 Timothy 6:1–21

    Real Wealth, Real Life

    True gain is godliness with contentment; so flee the love of money, pursue Christlike character, fight the good fight of the faith – and guard the gospel entrusted to you.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶
