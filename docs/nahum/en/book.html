A Refuge in the Day of Distress

The whole book of Nahum

The complete text of Nahum in the translation used in these devotionals.

Chapter 1

1An oracle concerning Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.

2A jealous God, one who takes vengeance, is the LORD;the LORD takes vengeance and is full of wrath.The LORD takes vengeance on his adversariesand holds it against his enemies.3The LORD is slow to anger and great in power,and the LORD will surely not acquit the guilty.In whirlwind and in storm is his way,and clouds are the dust of his feet.4He rebukes the sea and makes it dry,and all the rivers he dries up.Bashan and Carmel wither,and the blossom of Lebanon withers.5The mountains shake because of him,and the hills melt away;the earth rises up before his face,the world and all who live in it.6Before his fury who can stand?Who can rise up against his burning anger?His wrath pours out like fire,and the rocks are torn down by him.7The LORD is good,a refuge in the day of distress;he knows those who shelter in him.8But with a flood that passes overhe will make a full end of her place,and his enemies he will pursue into darkness.9What are you devising against the LORD?He is making a full end;distress will not rise up a second time.10For even if they are like tangled thornsand drunk as with their drink,they are devoured like dry stubble, completely.11From you has come outone who devises evil against the LORD,one who counsels what is worthless.12Thus says the LORD:Though they are at full strength and ever so many,even so they will be shorn and pass away.I have afflicted you;I will afflict you no more.13And now I will break his yoke from off youand tear your bonds apart.14But concerning you the LORD has commanded:Nothing more will be sown that bears your name.From the house of your gods I will cut offcarved image and cast image;I will make your grave,for you weigh nothing.

Chapter 2

1Look, on the mountainsthe feet of one who brings good news,who announces peace!Keep your festivals, Judah,pay your vows!For never again will the worthless onepass through you;he is cut off completely. (English Bibles: 1:15)2One who scatters has come up against you.Guard the rampart!Watch the road!Brace your loins!Gather all your strength! (English Bibles: 2:1; from here on subtract one from the verse number)3For the LORD restores the majesty of Jacoblike the majesty of Israel;for plunderers have plundered themand ruined their vine branches.4The shield of his warriors is reddened,the men of valour are clothed in scarlet;the chariots flash with the fire of steelon the day he makes ready,and the cypress spears are brandished.5In the streets the chariots rage,they rush to and fro in the squares;their appearance is like torches,like lightning they dart about.6He remembers his nobles;they stumble as they go;they hurry to her wall,and the protective cover is set up.7The gates of the rivers are opened,and the palace melts away.8And it is fixed: she is stripped, she is carried away,and her slave women moanlike the sound of doves,beating on their breasts.9Nineveh is like a pool of waterall her days;but now they are fleeing."Stop! Stop!" –but no one turns back.10Plunder silver! Plunder gold!There is no end to the treasure,wealth heavy with every precious thing.11Empty! Emptied! Laid waste!The heart melts, the knees knock,trembling is in all loins,and all their faces grow pale.12Where now is the lions' den,the pasture of the young lions,where the lion walked, the lioness,there the lion's cub,and no one made them afraid?13The lion tore enough for his cubsand strangled prey for his lionesses;he filled his holes with preyand his dens with torn flesh.14Look, I am against you– declares the LORD of hosts –I will burn her chariots in smoke,and the sword will devour your young lions;I will cut off your prey from the earth,and the voice of your messengerswill be heard no more.

Chapter 3

1Woe to the city of bloodshed!All of it is lies,full of plunder;the prey never departs.2The crack of the whipand the rumble of the wheel,the galloping horseand the jolting chariot!3Horsemen charging,the flame of the sword and the lightning of the spear,a multitude of slain,a heavy mass of corpses,no end to the bodies –they stumble over their bodies.4All this because of the many harlotries of the prostitute,lovely and charming,mistress of sorceries,who sells nations through her harlotriesand clans through her sorceries.5Look, I am against you– declares the LORD of hosts –I will lift your skirts over your faceand let the nations see your nakednessand the kingdoms your shame.6I will throw filth on you,treat you with contempt,and make you a spectacle.7And everyone who sees youwill flee from you and say:"Nineveh is devastated!Who will grieve for her?"Where shall I seek comforters for you?8Are you better than No-Amon,who sat by the channels of the Nile,with water all around her,whose rampart was the sea,her wall from the sea?9Cush was her strength, and Egypt,and there was no end;Put and the Libyanswere among your helpers.10Yet she too went into exile,she went into captivity;her little children too were dashed to piecesat the head of every street;for her honoured men they cast lots,and all her great men were bound in chains.11You too will be drunk,you will be hidden away;you too will seek a refuge from the enemy.12All your fortresses are fig treeswith first-ripe figs:if they are shaken,they fall into the mouth of the eater.13Look, your people are women in your midst;to your enemies the gates of your landare thrown wide open;fire has devoured your bars.14Draw water for the siege!Strengthen your fortresses!Go into the clayand tread the mortar!Take hold of the brick mould!15There the fire will devour you,the sword will cut you off;it will devour you like the hopper.Make yourself many like the hopper,make yourself many like the locust!16You have made your merchants more numerousthan the stars of the sky;the hopper sheds its skin and flies away.17Your guards are like the locust,your officials like a swarm of locuststhat settle in the wallson a cold day;when the sun rises, it flees,and its place is not known – where are they?18Your shepherds slumber, king of Assyria;your nobles lie down to rest;your people are scattered on the mountains,and there is no one to assemble them.19There is no easing for your fracture;your wound is grievous.All who hear the news of youclap their hands over you;for over whom has your evil not passedcontinually?
