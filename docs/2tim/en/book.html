Faithful to the End

The whole of Second Timothy

The complete text of Paul's second letter to Timothy in the translation used in these devotionals.

Chapter 1

1Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, in keeping with the promise of the life that is in Christ Jesus, 2to Timothy, my beloved child: grace, mercy and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

3I am grateful to God, whom I serve with a clear conscience as my forefathers did, when I remember you without ceasing in my prayers, night and day. 4I long to see you – I remember your tears – so that I may be filled with joy, 5for I have been reminded of the sincere faith that is in you, a faith that lived first in your grandmother Lois and in your mother Eunice, and I am convinced that it lives in you as well. 6For this reason I remind you to rekindle the gift of God that is in you through the laying on of my hands. 7For God did not give us a spirit of cowardice, but of power and of love and of self-control.

8Do not be ashamed, then, of the testimony about our Lord or of me, his prisoner; rather, share in hardship for the gospel, in the power of God, 9who saved us and called us with a holy calling, not on the basis of our works but on the basis of his own purpose and grace, which was given to us in Christ Jesus before eternal ages, 10but has now been revealed through the appearing of our Saviour Jesus Christ, who broke the power of death and brought life and imperishability to light through the gospel, 11for which I was appointed a herald and an apostle and a teacher. 12That is why I am suffering these things too; but I am not ashamed, for I know whom I have believed, and I am convinced that he is able to guard my entrusted deposit until that day. 13Hold to the example of sound words that you heard from me, in the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus. 14Guard the precious thing that has been entrusted to you, through the Holy Spirit who lives in us.

15You know that all who are in the province of Asia have turned away from me, among them Phygelus and Hermogenes. 16May the Lord grant mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, for he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chain. 17On the contrary, when he came to Rome, he searched hard for me and found me – 18may the Lord grant him to find mercy from the Lord on that day! – and you know better than I do how much he served in Ephesus.

Chapter 2

1You then, my child, be strengthened by the grace that is in Christ Jesus. 2And the things you heard from me through many witnesses – entrust these to faithful people who will be competent to teach others too. 3Share in hardship as a good soldier of Christ Jesus. 4No one serving as a soldier gets entangled in the affairs of everyday life, since he wants to please the one who enlisted him. 5Likewise, anyone who competes as an athlete is not crowned unless he competes according to the rules. 6The farmer who works hard ought to be the first to receive a share of the crops. 7Think about what I am saying, for the Lord will give you understanding in everything.

8Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, descended from David, according to my gospel. 9For this gospel I am enduring hardship, even to the point of being chained like a criminal. But the word of God is not chained. 10That is why I endure all things for the sake of the chosen, so that they too may obtain the salvation that is in Christ Jesus, with eternal glory. 11This is a trustworthy saying:

For if we died with him,we will also live with him;12if we endure,we will also reign with him;if we will deny him,he also will deny us;13if we are faithless,he remains faithful –for he cannot deny himself.

14Keep reminding them of these things, and solemnly charge them before the Lord not to fight about words; it is of no use and only ruins those who listen. 15Make every effort to present yourself to God as approved, a worker with nothing to be ashamed of, who handles the word of truth accurately. 16But avoid irreverent, empty chatter, for those who indulge in it will advance further and further into ungodliness, 17and their talk will spread like gangrene. Among them are Hymenaeus and Philetus, 18who have missed the mark concerning the truth by saying that the resurrection has already taken place, and they are overturning the faith of some. 19Yet the solid foundation laid by God stands firm, and it bears this seal: "The Lord knows who are his," and: "Everyone who names the name of the Lord must turn away from wrongdoing."

20In a large house you find not only vessels of gold and silver, but also vessels of wood and clay – some meant for honour, others for dishonour. 21So if anyone purifies himself from these, he will be a vessel meant for honour – consecrated, useful to the master, prepared for every good work. 22Flee from the desires of youth, and pursue righteousness, faith, love and peace, together with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart. 23Refuse foolish and uninstructed controversies, knowing that they give rise to quarrels. 24And a servant of the Lord must not quarrel, but be kind to everyone, able to teach, patient when wronged, 25instructing with gentleness those who oppose him, in the hope that God may grant them repentance that leads to the knowledge of the truth, 26and they will come to their senses and escape from the trap of the devil, having been taken captive by him for his will.

Chapter 3

1But understand this: in the last days difficult times will come. 2For people will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boastful, arrogant, blasphemers, disobedient to their parents, ungrateful, unholy, 3heartless, irreconcilable, slanderers, without self-restraint, brutal, haters of what is good, 4treacherous, reckless, puffed up, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God, 5having an outward form of godliness but denying its power. Turn away from such people! 6For among them are those who worm their way into households and take captive weak women, who are weighed down with sins and driven by all kinds of desires, 7always learning and never able to come to the knowledge of the truth. 8Just as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so these also oppose the truth: people corrupted in mind, who have failed the test regarding the faith. 9But they will not get very far, for their folly will become plain to everyone, as happened with those two.

10You, however, have closely followed my teaching, my way of life, my purpose, my faith, my patience, my love, my endurance, 11my persecutions and my sufferings, the things that happened to me in Antioch, in Iconium and in Lystra. What persecutions I bore! And the Lord rescued me from all of them. 12Indeed, all who want to live in godliness in Christ Jesus will be persecuted. 13But evil people and impostors will go from bad to worse, deceiving and being deceived. 14But you, remain in what you have learned and have become convinced of, knowing from whom you learned it, 15and that from childhood you have known the sacred writings, which can give you the wisdom that leads to salvation through faith in Christ Jesus. 16All Scripture is God-breathed and useful for teaching, for reproof, for correction, for training in righteousness, 17so that the man of God may be fully capable, equipped for every good work.

Chapter 4

1I solemnly charge you before God and Christ Jesus, who is going to judge the living and the dead, and by his appearing and his kingdom: 2proclaim the word; keep at it whether the time is right or not; reprove, rebuke, encourage, with all patience and teaching. 3For the time will come when people will not put up with sound teaching, but, following their own desires, will pile up teachers for themselves because their ears itch, 4and they will turn their ears away from the truth and wander off to myths. 5But you, be sober-minded in everything, endure hardship, do the work of an evangelist, carry out your service fully.

6For already I am being poured out as a drink offering, and the time for my departure is here. 7I have fought the good fight, I have run the race to the end, I have kept the faith. 8What remains is this: the crown of righteousness is stored up for me, which the Lord, the righteous judge, will give me on that day – and not to me only, but to all who have loved his appearing.

9Make every effort to come to me soon. 10For Demas has abandoned me, because he loved this present age, and has gone to Thessalonica; Crescens has gone to Galatia, Titus to Dalmatia. 11Only Luke is with me. Pick up Mark and bring him along, for he is useful to me for service. 12I have sent Tychicus to Ephesus. 13When you come, bring the cloak that I left with Carpus in Troas, and the books, especially the parchments. 14Alexander the metalworker did me much harm – the Lord will repay him according to his works. 15You too must be on your guard against him, for he strongly opposed our words.

16At my first defence no one stood by me, but all abandoned me – may it not be counted against them! 17But the Lord stood by me and gave me strength, so that through me the proclamation might be fully carried out and all the nations might hear it; and I was rescued from the mouth of the lion. 18The Lord will rescue me from every evil deed and will save me for his heavenly kingdom. To him be the glory for ever and ever. Amen.

19Greet Prisca and Aquila and the household of Onesiphorus. 20Erastus stayed in Corinth, and I left Trophimus sick in Miletus. 21Make every effort to come before winter. Eubulus greets you, as do Pudens and Linus and Claudia and all the brothers and sisters.

22The Lord be with your spirit. Grace be with you all.
