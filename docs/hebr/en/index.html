How to use this booklet

  1. Set aside about 15 to 20 minutes for each day, ideally somewhere quiet.
  2. Read the Scripture passage first, slowly – aloud, if you can. It stands at the start of each devotional.
  3. “Digging Deeper” takes you into the context and the language of the original text. “Living It Out” asks what it means for you.
  4. Take your time with the questions. You may want to write your answers down.
  5. Close with the prayer – and with your own words.
