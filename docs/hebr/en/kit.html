Eyes Fixed on Jesus

Small-group kit

Thirteen sessions for home groups and small groups – one per chapter, 75 to 90 minutes each. Each session builds on the devotionals.

Leader's guide as PDF (A4) All slides as PDF

  1. Session 1 · Hebrews 1:1–14

    The Final Word

    God has spoken his final word in his Son, who radiates his glory, carries all things, has made purification for sins and now reigns far above the angels – so we can rest in his finished work and anchor our lives in the one who remains the same.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  2. Session 10 · Hebrews 10:1–39

    The New and Living Way

    By one offering Christ has made perfect for all time those who are made holy; so let us approach God, hold fast to our hope and consider one another – and not throw away our confidence, for the one who is coming will come.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  3. Session 11 · Hebrews 11:1–40

    By Faith

    Faith treats God's promises as a title deed in our hand: it makes us pilgrims on the way to the city whose builder is God, it values the reproach of the Christ above the treasures of Egypt, and it holds on – whether God delivers us or carries us through suffering.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  4. Session 12 · Hebrews 12:1–29

    The Race and the Mountain

    With our eyes fixed on Jesus we run the race with endurance and accept the Father's discipline as love; and because we have approached Mount Zion, not Sinai, we receive a kingdom that cannot be shaken and worship God with gratitude, reverence and awe.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  5. Session 13 · Hebrews 13:1–25

    The Great Shepherd

    Grateful worship in a kingdom that cannot be shaken becomes practical love, contentment and the courage to go out to Jesus outside the camp; we offer God the sacrifices of praise and sharing – and the God of peace, who brought up the great shepherd from the dead, himself equips us to do his will.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  6. Session 2 · Hebrews 2:1–18

    Do Not Drift

    Because the Son is so great, we must not drift away from a salvation this great: Jesus became one of us, tasted death for everyone by the grace of God, broke the power of the devil, and is now the merciful and faithful high priest who helps us when we are tested.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  7. Session 3 · Hebrews 3:1–19

    Today

    Jesus, the Son over God's house, is greater than Moses, the faithful servant in it – so we consider him closely, do not harden our hearts when he speaks today, and encourage one another every day, because holding fast to the end shows that we have truly become partners of Christ.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  8. Session 4 · Hebrews 4:1–16

    The Promise of Rest

    A Sabbath rest remains for the people of God: we enter it by faith, not by our own works, under the living word that lays our hearts bare – and we can approach the throne of grace with confidence, because our great high priest sympathizes with our weaknesses.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  9. Session 5 · Hebrews 5:1–14

    Our Understanding High Priest

    Jesus did not take the honour of high priest for himself: called by God, he learned obedience through what he suffered and became the source of eternal salvation – and he calls us on from milk to solid food, to a faith trained by practice.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  10. Session 6 · Hebrews 6:1–20

    The Soul's Anchor

    Don't stand still at the foundation: the author warns earnestly against falling away, yet he is convinced of better things for his readers – and our hope rests on God's promise and God's oath, an anchor fixed behind the curtain where Jesus has gone ahead of us.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  11. Session 7 · Hebrews 7:1–28

    A Priest for Ever

    Melchizedek, without genealogy and greater than Abraham, points to Jesus: a priest not by descent but by the power of an indestructible life and by God's oath, who always lives to intercede for us and who offered himself once for all.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  12. Session 8 · Hebrews 8:1–13

    Shadow and Reality

    We have a high priest who has sat down in the true tent and is mediator of a better covenant, whose promises rest on God's own “I will”: he writes his laws on our hearts and remembers our sins no more.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶

  13. Session 9 · Hebrews 9:1–28

    Through His Own Blood

    The earthly tent showed that the way into God's presence was not yet open; Christ entered the sanctuary once for all through his own blood, cleanses our conscience from dead works so that we may worship the living God, appears now before God for us and will appear a second time for salvation.

    Leader's guide → Present ▶
