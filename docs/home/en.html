How it works

  1. Choose a series and read one devotional a day.
  2. When you have finished, tap “Mark as read”.
  3. From the next day on, this page opens automatically at the next devotional.
  4. Your progress is stored only on this device – nowhere else.

Tip: add this page to your home screen. It then works like an app – even offline – and your progress is kept safe.
