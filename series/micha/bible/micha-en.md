# The Book of Micah

## Chapter 1

**1** The word of the LORD that came to Micah of Moresheth in the days of Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, which he saw concerning Samaria and Jerusalem.

> **2** Hear, you peoples, all of you!
> Listen, O earth, and all that fills it!
> Let the Lord, the LORD, be a witness against you,
> the Lord from his holy temple.
> **3** For look: the LORD is coming out of his place;
> he comes down and treads on the high places of the earth.
> **4** The mountains melt beneath him,
> and the valleys split open,
> like wax before the fire,
> like water poured down a slope.
> **5** All this is for the rebellion of Jacob
> and for the sins of the house of Israel.
> What is the rebellion of Jacob?
> Is it not Samaria?
> And what are the high places of Judah?
> Is it not Jerusalem?
> **6** So I will make Samaria a heap of rubble in the open field,
> a place for planting vineyards;
> I will pour her stones down into the valley
> and lay bare her foundations.
> **7** All her carved images will be smashed,
> all her wages will be burned with fire,
> and all her idols I will make a desolation;
> for from the wages of a prostitute she gathered them,
> and to the wages of a prostitute they will return.

> **8** Because of this I will lament and wail;
> I will go stripped and naked;
> I will make a lament like the jackals
> and a mourning like the ostriches.
> **9** For her wounds are incurable;
> it has come as far as Judah,
> it has reached the gate of my people,
> as far as Jerusalem.
> **10** In Gath do not tell it;
> do not weep at all.
> In Beth-leaphrah
> roll yourself in the dust.
> **11** Pass on your way,
> inhabitant of Shaphir, in nakedness and shame.
> The inhabitant of Zaanan
> has not come out.
> The lament of Beth-ezel –
> it will take from you its standing place.
> **12** For the inhabitant of Maroth
> waits in anguish for good,
> for evil has come down from the LORD
> to the gate of Jerusalem.
> **13** Harness the chariot to the steed,
> inhabitant of Lachish!
> She was the beginning of sin for Daughter Zion,
> for in you were found the rebellions of Israel.
> **14** Therefore you will give parting gifts
> to Moresheth-gath;
> the houses of Achzib will be a deceit
> to the kings of Israel.
> **15** Once more I will bring the one who takes possession to you,
> inhabitant of Mareshah;
> as far as Adullam
> the glory of Israel will come.
> **16** Make yourself bald and shave your head
> for the children of your delight;
> make your baldness as wide as the vulture's,
> for they have gone from you into exile.

## Chapter 2

> **1** Woe to those who devise wickedness
> and work evil on their beds!
> In the light of morning they carry it out,
> because it is in the power of their hand.
> **2** They covet fields and seize them,
> and houses, and take them away;
> they oppress a man and his house,
> a person and his inheritance.
> **3** Therefore thus says the LORD:
> Look, against this family I am devising evil,
> from which you cannot remove your necks;
> and you will not walk with heads held high,
> for it will be a time of evil.
> **4** On that day they will take up a taunt-song against you
> and wail a bitter lament:
> "It is done!" one says.
> "We are utterly ruined!
> He exchanges the portion of my people.
> How he takes it away from me!
> To the renegade he divides up our fields."
> **5** Therefore you will have no one
> to cast the measuring line by lot
> in the assembly of the LORD.

> **6** "Do not preach!" – so they preach.
> "One should not preach about such things;
> disgrace will not overtake us."
> **7** Should this be said, house of Jacob:
> "Is the Spirit of the LORD impatient?
> Are these his deeds?"
> Do my words not do good
> to the one who walks uprightly?
> **8** But lately my people has risen up as an enemy.
> From off the garment you strip the cloak
> from those who pass by in trust,
> like men coming home from war.
> **9** The women of my people you drive out,
> each from the house she delights in;
> from her little children you take away
> my splendour for ever.
> **10** Get up and go!
> For this is not the resting place;
> because of uncleanness it brings destruction,
> a grievous destruction.
> **11** If a man came walking in wind and falsehood
> and lied: "I will preach to you about wine and strong drink!" –
> he would be the preacher for this people.

> **12** I will surely gather all of you, Jacob;
> I will surely assemble the remnant of Israel.
> I will put them together like sheep in a fold,
> like a flock in the midst of its pasture;
> they will be noisy with people.
> **13** The one who breaks open goes up before them;
> they break through, pass through the gate
> and go out by it.
> Their king passes on before them,
> and the LORD at their head.

## Chapter 3

> **1** And I said:
> Hear, please, you heads of Jacob
> and rulers of the house of Israel!
> Is it not for you to know justice?
> **2** You who hate good and love evil,
> who tear their skin from off them
> and their flesh from off their bones;
> **3** who eat the flesh of my people
> and strip their skin from off them,
> break their bones in pieces
> and chop them up like meat for the pot,
> like flesh in the cauldron.
> **4** Then they will cry out to the LORD,
> but he will not answer them;
> he will hide his face from them at that time,
> because they have made their deeds evil.

> **5** Thus says the LORD concerning the prophets
> who lead my people astray,
> who, when they have something to bite with their teeth,
> call out "Peace!",
> but whoever puts nothing into their mouths,
> against him they sanctify war:
> **6** Therefore it will be night for you, without vision,
> and darkness for you, without divination.
> The sun will go down on the prophets,
> and the day will grow dark over them.
> **7** The seers will be ashamed
> and the diviners disgraced;
> they will all cover their lips,
> for there is no answer from God.
> **8** But as for me, I am filled with power,
> with the Spirit of the LORD,
> with justice and with might,
> to declare to Jacob his rebellion
> and to Israel his sin.

> **9** Hear this, please, you heads of the house of Jacob
> and rulers of the house of Israel,
> who abhor justice
> and twist everything that is straight,
> **10** who build Zion with blood
> and Jerusalem with injustice!
> **11** Her heads judge for a bribe,
> her priests teach for a price,
> and her prophets divine for money;
> yet they lean on the LORD and say:
> "Is not the LORD in our midst?
> No evil will come upon us."
> **12** Therefore, because of you,
> Zion will be ploughed as a field,
> Jerusalem will become heaps of ruins,
> and the mountain of the house a wooded height.

## Chapter 4

> **1** And it will come to pass in the last days
> that the mountain of the house of the LORD
> will stand firm as the highest of the mountains
> and be lifted up above the hills,
> and peoples will stream to it.
> **2** Many nations will come and say:
> "Come, let us go up to the mountain of the LORD,
> to the house of the God of Jacob,
> so that he may teach us his ways
> and we may walk in his paths."
> For from Zion instruction will go out,
> and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.
> **3** He will judge between many peoples
> and settle disputes for mighty nations far away.
> They will beat their swords into ploughshares
> and their spears into pruning knives.
> Nation will not lift up sword against nation,
> and they will no longer learn war.
> **4** They will sit, each under his vine
> and under his fig tree,
> and no one will make them afraid;
> for the mouth of the LORD of hosts has spoken.
> **5** For all the peoples walk,
> each in the name of its god,
> but we will walk in the name of the LORD our God
> for ever and ever.

> **6** On that day – declares the LORD –
> I will gather the lame
> and assemble the outcast,
> and the one on whom I brought evil.
> **7** I will make the lame a remnant,
> and the one driven far away a mighty nation;
> and the LORD will reign over them on Mount Zion
> from now on and for ever.
> **8** And you, tower of the flock,
> hill of Daughter Zion,
> to you it will come:
> the former dominion will come,
> the kingdom of Daughter Jerusalem.

> **9** Now, why do you cry out so loudly?
> Is there no king in you?
> Has your counsellor perished,
> that pain has seized you like a woman giving birth?
> **10** Writhe and push, Daughter Zion,
> like a woman giving birth!
> For now you will go out of the city
> and live in the open field;
> you will come as far as Babylon.
> There you will be rescued;
> there the LORD will redeem you
> from the hand of your enemies.
> **11** And now many nations
> have gathered against you,
> who say: "Let her be defiled,
> and let our eyes gloat over Zion!"
> **12** But they do not know the plans of the LORD,
> and they do not understand his counsel:
> that he has gathered them like sheaves to the threshing floor.
> **13** Get up and thresh, Daughter Zion!
> For I will make your horn iron,
> and your hoofs I will make bronze;
> you will crush many peoples.
> And I will devote their gain to the LORD,
> their wealth to the Lord of all the earth.

> **14** Now gather your troops, daughter of troops!
> A siege has been laid against us;
> with the staff they strike the judge of Israel
> on the cheek. *(English Bibles: 5:1)*

## Chapter 5

> **1** But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah,
> too small to be among the clans of Judah,
> from you one will come out for me
> to be ruler in Israel;
> his origins are from of old,
> from ancient days. *(English Bibles: 5:2; from here on add one to the verse number)*
> **2** Therefore he will give them up
> until the time when she who is in labour has given birth;
> then the rest of his brothers will return
> to the children of Israel.
> **3** He will stand and shepherd
> in the strength of the LORD,
> in the majesty of the name of the LORD his God.
> And they will dwell secure,
> for now he will be great
> to the ends of the earth.
> **4** And this one will be peace.
> When Assyria comes into our land
> and when he treads in our fortresses,
> we will raise against him seven shepherds
> and eight princes of men.
> **5** They will shepherd the land of Assyria with the sword,
> and the land of Nimrod at its gates.
> And he will rescue us from Assyria
> when he comes into our land
> and when he treads within our borders.

> **6** And the remnant of Jacob will be
> in the midst of many peoples
> like dew from the LORD,
> like showers on the grass,
> which does not wait for a man
> nor hope for the children of men.
> **7** And the remnant of Jacob will be among the nations,
> in the midst of many peoples,
> like a lion among the animals of the forest,
> like a young lion among the flocks of sheep,
> which, if it passes through, tramples and tears,
> and there is no one to rescue.
> **8** Your hand will be lifted high over your adversaries,
> and all your enemies will be cut off.

> **9** And it will come to pass on that day – declares the LORD –
> that I will cut off your horses from among you
> and make your chariots perish.
> **10** I will cut off the cities of your land
> and tear down all your fortresses.
> **11** I will cut off sorceries from your hand,
> and you will have no more fortune-tellers.
> **12** I will cut off your carved images
> and your sacred pillars from among you,
> and you will no longer bow down
> to the work of your hands.
> **13** I will uproot your Asherah poles from among you
> and destroy your cities.
> **14** And in anger and in wrath I will execute vengeance
> on the nations that did not listen.

## Chapter 6

> **1** Hear, please, what the LORD says:
> Get up, plead the case before the mountains,
> and let the hills hear your voice!
> **2** Hear, you mountains, the case of the LORD,
> you enduring ones, foundations of the earth!
> For the LORD has a case against his people,
> and with Israel he will argue it.
> **3** My people, what have I done to you?
> How have I wearied you? Answer me!
> **4** For I brought you up from the land of Egypt,
> and from the house of slaves I redeemed you;
> and I sent before you
> Moses, Aaron and Miriam.
> **5** My people, remember
> what Balak king of Moab planned,
> and what Balaam son of Beor answered him –
> from Shittim to Gilgal –
> so that you may know the righteous acts of the LORD.

> **6** With what shall I come before the LORD,
> and bow down before God on high?
> Shall I come before him with burnt offerings,
> with calves a year old?
> **7** Will the LORD be pleased with thousands of rams,
> with ten thousand rivers of oil?
> Shall I give my firstborn for my rebellion,
> the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?
> **8** He has told you, O man, what is good,
> and what the LORD seeks from you:
> only to do justice,
> to love faithful love,
> and to walk humbly with your God.

> **9** The voice of the LORD calls out to the city –
> and it is wisdom to see your name:
> Hear the rod, and the one who appointed it!
> **10** Are there still in the house of the wicked
> treasures of wickedness,
> and the short measure that is cursed?
> **11** Can I count anyone pure with wicked scales
> and with a bag of deceitful weights?
> **12** Her rich men are full of violence,
> her inhabitants speak falsehood,
> and their tongue is deceit in their mouth.
> **13** So I too have begun to strike you,
> to make you desolate because of your sins.
> **14** You will eat, but not be satisfied,
> and the hunger will be inside you;
> you will put things aside, but not save them,
> and what you save I will give to the sword.
> **15** You will sow, but not reap;
> you will tread olives, but not anoint yourself with oil,
> and grapes for new wine, but not drink wine.
> **16** The statutes of Omri are kept,
> and all the practices of the house of Ahab,
> and you walk in their counsels;
> so I will make you a desolation,
> and her inhabitants something to hiss at;
> and you will bear the reproach of my people.

## Chapter 7

> **1** Woe is me! For I have become
> like one gathering summer fruit,
> like one gleaning after the grape harvest:
> there is no cluster to eat,
> no early fig that my soul longs for.
> **2** The faithful one has perished from the land,
> and there is no one upright among people.
> They all lie in wait for blood;
> each hunts his brother with a net.
> **3** Their hands are on evil, to do it well:
> the official asks, and the judge – for a payment;
> and the great man speaks out the craving of his soul;
> and so they weave it together.
> **4** The best of them is like a briar,
> the most upright worse than a thorn hedge.
> The day of your watchmen, your punishment, has come;
> now their confusion will be.
> **5** Do not trust a friend,
> do not rely on a close companion;
> from her who lies in your embrace
> guard the doors of your mouth.
> **6** For son treats father with contempt,
> daughter rises up against her mother,
> daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law;
> a man's enemies are the people of his own house.
> **7** But as for me, I will watch for the LORD;
> I will wait for the God of my salvation;
> my God will hear me.

> **8** Do not rejoice over me, my enemy!
> When I have fallen, I rise;
> when I sit in darkness,
> the LORD is light for me.
> **9** The rage of the LORD I will bear,
> for I have sinned against him,
> until he pleads my case
> and brings about justice for me.
> He will bring me out into the light;
> I will see his righteousness.
> **10** And my enemy will see it,
> and shame will cover her
> who said to me: "Where is he, the LORD your God?"
> My eyes will look on her;
> now she will be trampled down
> like mud in the streets.
> **11** A day for building your walls!
> On that day the boundary will be pushed far out.
> **12** On that day they will come to you,
> from Assyria and the cities of Egypt,
> and from Egypt to the River,
> from sea to sea and from mountain to mountain.
> **13** But the earth will become a desolation
> because of those who live in it,
> as the fruit of their deeds.

> **14** Shepherd your people with your staff,
> the flock of your inheritance,
> who live alone in a forest,
> in the midst of a fruitful land;
> let them graze in Bashan and Gilead
> as in ancient days.
> **15** As in the days when you came out of the land of Egypt,
> I will show him wonders.
> **16** Nations will see and be ashamed
> of all their might;
> they will lay a hand on their mouth;
> their ears will be deaf.
> **17** They will lick dust like the snake,
> like the creatures that crawl on the earth;
> they will come trembling out of their strongholds;
> to the LORD our God they will come in dread,
> and they will fear you.

> **18** Who is a God like you,
> who bears away guilt
> and passes over rebellion
> for the remnant of his inheritance?
> He does not hold on to his anger for ever,
> for he delights in faithful love.
> **19** He will again have compassion on us;
> he will tread our guilt underfoot.
> And you will cast all their sins
> into the depths of the sea.
> **20** You will give truth to Jacob,
> faithful love to Abraham,
> as you swore to our fathers
> from days of old.
