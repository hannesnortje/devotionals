# The Book of Habakkuk

## Chapter 1

**1** The oracle that Habakkuk the prophet saw.

> **2** How long, LORD, shall I cry for help,
> and you do not hear?
> I cry out to you, "Violence!"
> and you do not save.
> **3** Why do you make me see wickedness,
> and why do you look on misery?
> Destruction and violence are before me;
> there is strife, and contention rises up.
> **4** Therefore instruction grows numb,
> and justice never comes forth.
> For the wicked surrounds the righteous;
> therefore justice comes forth twisted.

> **5** See among the nations, and look;
> be astonished – be astounded!
> For I am doing a work in your days
> that you would not believe if it were told.
> **6** For look, I am raising up the Chaldeans,
> that bitter and impetuous nation,
> who march across the breadth of the earth
> to take possession of dwellings that are not their own.
> **7** Terrible and dreadful is he;
> from himself come forth his justice and his dignity.
> **8** His horses are swifter than leopards
> and fiercer than wolves at evening.
> His horsemen gallop on,
> his horsemen come from far away;
> they fly like a vulture that hurries to devour.
> **9** All of them come for violence;
> the set of their faces is forward,
> and he gathers captives like sand.
> **10** He mocks at kings,
> and rulers are a joke to him.
> He laughs at every fortress;
> he heaps up dust and captures it.
> **11** Then he sweeps on like the wind and passes by,
> and he becomes guilty –
> he whose strength is his god.

> **12** Are you not from of old, LORD,
> my God, my Holy One?
> We will not die.
> LORD, you have appointed him for judgment;
> O Rock, you have established him to reprove.
> **13** Your eyes are too pure to see evil,
> and you cannot look on misery.
> Why do you look on the treacherous
> and keep silent when the wicked swallows up
> one more righteous than he?
> **14** You make people like the fish of the sea,
> like crawling things that have no ruler.
> **15** He brings them all up with a hook,
> he drags them away in his net
> and gathers them in his dragnet;
> so he is glad and rejoices.
> **16** Therefore he sacrifices to his net
> and burns incense to his dragnet,
> for through them his portion is fat
> and his food is rich.
> **17** Is he then to keep emptying his net
> and go on killing nations without sparing?

## Chapter 2

> **1** I will stand at my guard post
> and take my place on the rampart;
> I will watch to see what he will say to me
> and what I shall answer concerning my reproof.

**2** Then the LORD answered me and said:

> Write down the vision
> and make it plain on the tablets,
> so that whoever reads it may run.
> **3** For the vision is still for the appointed time;
> it pants toward the end, and it will not deceive.
> If it lingers, wait for it,
> for it will surely come; it will not delay.
> **4** Look, he is puffed up – his soul is not upright within him;
> but the righteous will live by his faithfulness.
> **5** And moreover, wine is treacherous;
> an arrogant man does not stay at home.
> He opens his throat wide like the grave;
> he is like death and is never sated.
> He gathers to himself all the nations
> and assembles to himself all the peoples.

> **6** Will not all of these take up a taunt against him,
> a mocking song, riddles about him, and say:
> Woe to the one who heaps up what is not his own –
> how long? –
> and loads himself heavy with pledges!
> **7** Will not those who bite you rise up suddenly,
> and those who make you tremble wake up,
> and you will become plunder for them?
> **8** Because you have plundered many nations,
> all who are left of the peoples will plunder you,
> because of human blood and violence to the land,
> to the city and all who live in it.

> **9** Woe to the one who gains evil gain for his house,
> to set his nest on high,
> to be snatched from the grip of evil!
> **10** You have counselled shame for your house
> by cutting off many peoples,
> and you are sinning against your own life.
> **11** For a stone from the wall will cry out,
> and a rafter from the woodwork will answer it.

> **12** Woe to the one who builds a town with blood
> and founds a city on injustice!
> **13** Look, is it not from the LORD of hosts
> that peoples toil only for the fire
> and nations weary themselves for nothing?
> **14** For the earth will be filled
> with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD,
> as the waters cover the sea.

> **15** Woe to the one who makes his neighbour drink,
> mixing in your wrath and making him drunk as well,
> in order to look on their nakedness!
> **16** You are sated with disgrace instead of glory.
> Drink, you too, and show your foreskin!
> The cup in the LORD's right hand will come around to you,
> and utter disgrace will come upon your glory.
> **17** For the violence done to Lebanon will cover you,
> and the destruction of the beasts that terrified them,
> because of human blood and violence to the land,
> to the city and all who live in it.

> **18** What use is a carved image,
> that its maker has carved it,
> a cast image and a teacher of lies?
> For its maker trusts in what he himself has formed
> when he makes mute idols.
> **19** Woe to the one who says to wood, "Wake up!"
> "Awake!" to a silent stone.
> Can it teach?
> Look, it is overlaid with gold and silver,
> and there is no breath at all inside it.
> **20** But the LORD is in his holy temple –
> silence before him, all the earth!

## Chapter 3

**1** A prayer of Habakkuk the prophet, according to *shigionot*.

> **2** LORD, I have heard the report of you;
> I fear, LORD, your work.
> In the midst of the years, bring it to life;
> in the midst of the years, make it known.
> In turmoil, remember compassion.

> **3** God came from Teman,
> the Holy One from Mount Paran.
> *Sela*
> His splendour covered the heavens,
> and the earth was full of his praise.
> **4** His brightness was like the light;
> rays broke from his hand,
> and there his power was veiled.
> **5** Before him went pestilence,
> and plague came forth at his feet.
> **6** He stood and measured the earth;
> he looked and made the nations start.
> The everlasting mountains were shattered,
> the ancient hills sank low;
> the ancient ways are his.
> **7** I saw the tents of Cushan under calamity;
> the tent curtains of the land of Midian trembled.

> **8** Was it against the rivers that the LORD burned?
> Was your anger against the rivers,
> or your rage against the sea,
> when you rode on your horses,
> your chariots of salvation?
> **9** Your bow was laid bare, utterly bare;
> sworn were the rods – a word.
> *Sela*
> You split the earth into rivers.
> **10** The mountains saw you and writhed;
> a torrent of water passed over.
> The deep gave its voice;
> it lifted its hands on high.
> **11** Sun and moon stood still in their dwelling
> at the light of your arrows as they sped,
> at the brightness of the flash of your spear.
> **12** In fury you marched through the earth;
> in anger you threshed the nations.
> **13** You went out for the salvation of your people,
> for the salvation of your anointed.
> You crushed the head of the house of the wicked,
> laying bare the foundation up to the neck.
> *Sela*
> **14** With his own rods you pierced the head of his warriors,
> who stormed out to scatter me;
> their exulting was as if to devour
> the poor in a hiding place.
> **15** You trod the sea with your horses,
> the surging of many waters.

> **16** I heard, and my insides trembled;
> at the sound my lips quivered.
> Rot entered my bones,
> and where I stand I tremble.
> Yet I will wait quietly for the day of distress
> to come up against the people who invade us.

> **17** Though the fig tree does not blossom
> and there is no fruit on the vines,
> though the olive crop fails
> and the terraced fields yield no food,
> though the flock is cut off from the pen
> and there are no cattle in the stalls –
> **18** yet I will exult in the LORD,
> I will rejoice in the God of my salvation.
> **19** The LORD, the Lord, is my strength;
> he makes my feet like those of the deer
> and lets me tread on my high places.
