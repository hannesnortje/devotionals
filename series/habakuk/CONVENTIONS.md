## Habakkuk specifics
- Verse numbers: Hebrew and English Bibles number Habakkuk the same.
- Hannes's docs quote the AF 1933/53 text – replace all Habakkuk quotations with our translation. Day 1's doc opens with a welcome and ends with a progress table: keep the welcome (shortened), drop the table and all chat lines.
- Day 6 (the whole of ch. 3) closes the series; 3:17–19 gives the series title.
- 2:4 is quoted in Romans 1:17, Galatians 3:11 and Hebrews 10:38 – NT quotations must match our own NT files where they exist (series/hebr/bible/hebr-{lang}.md for Heb 10:37–38); otherwise our own short rendering.
