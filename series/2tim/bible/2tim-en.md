# The Second Letter to Timothy

## Chapter 1

**1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, in keeping with the promise of the life that is in Christ Jesus,
**2** to Timothy, my beloved child: grace, mercy and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

**3** I am grateful to God, whom I serve with a clear conscience as my forefathers did, when I remember you without ceasing in my prayers, night and day.
**4** I long to see you – I remember your tears – so that I may be filled with joy,
**5** for I have been reminded of the sincere faith that is in you, a faith that lived first in your grandmother Lois and in your mother Eunice, and I am convinced that it lives in you as well.
**6** For this reason I remind you to rekindle the gift of God that is in you through the laying on of my hands.
**7** For God did not give us a spirit of cowardice, but of power and of love and of self-control.

**8** Do not be ashamed, then, of the testimony about our Lord or of me, his prisoner; rather, share in hardship for the gospel, in the power of God,
**9** who saved us and called us with a holy calling, not on the basis of our works but on the basis of his own purpose and grace, which was given to us in Christ Jesus before eternal ages,
**10** but has now been revealed through the appearing of our Saviour Jesus Christ, who broke the power of death and brought life and imperishability to light through the gospel,
**11** for which I was appointed a herald and an apostle and a teacher.
**12** That is why I am suffering these things too; but I am not ashamed, for I know whom I have believed, and I am convinced that he is able to guard my entrusted deposit until that day.
**13** Hold to the example of sound words that you heard from me, in the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.
**14** Guard the precious thing that has been entrusted to you, through the Holy Spirit who lives in us.

**15** You know that all who are in the province of Asia have turned away from me, among them Phygelus and Hermogenes.
**16** May the Lord grant mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, for he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chain.
**17** On the contrary, when he came to Rome, he searched hard for me and found me –
**18** may the Lord grant him to find mercy from the Lord on that day! – and you know better than I do how much he served in Ephesus.

## Chapter 2

**1** You then, my child, be strengthened by the grace that is in Christ Jesus.
**2** And the things you heard from me through many witnesses – entrust these to faithful people who will be competent to teach others too.
**3** Share in hardship as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.
**4** No one serving as a soldier gets entangled in the affairs of everyday life, since he wants to please the one who enlisted him.
**5** Likewise, anyone who competes as an athlete is not crowned unless he competes according to the rules.
**6** The farmer who works hard ought to be the first to receive a share of the crops.
**7** Think about what I am saying, for the Lord will give you understanding in everything.

**8** Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, descended from David, according to my gospel.
**9** For this gospel I am enduring hardship, even to the point of being chained like a criminal. But the word of God is not chained.
**10** That is why I endure all things for the sake of the chosen, so that they too may obtain the salvation that is in Christ Jesus, with eternal glory.
**11** This is a trustworthy saying:

> For if we died with him,
> we will also live with him;
> **12** if we endure,
> we will also reign with him;
> if we will deny him,
> he also will deny us;
> **13** if we are faithless,
> he remains faithful –
> for he cannot deny himself.

**14** Keep reminding them of these things, and solemnly charge them before the Lord not to fight about words; it is of no use and only ruins those who listen.
**15** Make every effort to present yourself to God as approved, a worker with nothing to be ashamed of, who handles the word of truth accurately.
**16** But avoid irreverent, empty chatter, for those who indulge in it will advance further and further into ungodliness,
**17** and their talk will spread like gangrene. Among them are Hymenaeus and Philetus,
**18** who have missed the mark concerning the truth by saying that the resurrection has already taken place, and they are overturning the faith of some.
**19** Yet the solid foundation laid by God stands firm, and it bears this seal: "The Lord knows who are his," and: "Everyone who names the name of the Lord must turn away from wrongdoing."

**20** In a large house you find not only vessels of gold and silver, but also vessels of wood and clay – some meant for honour, others for dishonour.
**21** So if anyone purifies himself from these, he will be a vessel meant for honour – consecrated, useful to the master, prepared for every good work.
**22** Flee from the desires of youth, and pursue righteousness, faith, love and peace, together with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart.
**23** Refuse foolish and uninstructed controversies, knowing that they give rise to quarrels.
**24** And a servant of the Lord must not quarrel, but be kind to everyone, able to teach, patient when wronged,
**25** instructing with gentleness those who oppose him, in the hope that God may grant them repentance that leads to the knowledge of the truth,
**26** and they will come to their senses and escape from the trap of the devil, having been taken captive by him for his will.

## Chapter 3

**1** But understand this: in the last days difficult times will come.
**2** For people will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boastful, arrogant, blasphemers, disobedient to their parents, ungrateful, unholy,
**3** heartless, irreconcilable, slanderers, without self-restraint, brutal, haters of what is good,
**4** treacherous, reckless, puffed up, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God,
**5** having an outward form of godliness but denying its power. Turn away from such people!
**6** For among them are those who worm their way into households and take captive weak women, who are weighed down with sins and driven by all kinds of desires,
**7** always learning and never able to come to the knowledge of the truth.
**8** Just as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so these also oppose the truth: people corrupted in mind, who have failed the test regarding the faith.
**9** But they will not get very far, for their folly will become plain to everyone, as happened with those two.

**10** You, however, have closely followed my teaching, my way of life, my purpose, my faith, my patience, my love, my endurance,
**11** my persecutions and my sufferings, the things that happened to me in Antioch, in Iconium and in Lystra. What persecutions I bore! And the Lord rescued me from all of them.
**12** Indeed, all who want to live in godliness in Christ Jesus will be persecuted.
**13** But evil people and impostors will go from bad to worse, deceiving and being deceived.
**14** But you, remain in what you have learned and have become convinced of, knowing from whom you learned it,
**15** and that from childhood you have known the sacred writings, which can give you the wisdom that leads to salvation through faith in Christ Jesus.
**16** All Scripture is God-breathed and useful for teaching, for reproof, for correction, for training in righteousness,
**17** so that the man of God may be fully capable, equipped for every good work.

## Chapter 4

**1** I solemnly charge you before God and Christ Jesus, who is going to judge the living and the dead, and by his appearing and his kingdom:
**2** proclaim the word; keep at it whether the time is right or not; reprove, rebuke, encourage, with all patience and teaching.
**3** For the time will come when people will not put up with sound teaching, but, following their own desires, will pile up teachers for themselves because their ears itch,
**4** and they will turn their ears away from the truth and wander off to myths.
**5** But you, be sober-minded in everything, endure hardship, do the work of an evangelist, carry out your service fully.

**6** For already I am being poured out as a drink offering, and the time for my departure is here.
**7** I have fought the good fight, I have run the race to the end, I have kept the faith.
**8** What remains is this: the crown of righteousness is stored up for me, which the Lord, the righteous judge, will give me on that day – and not to me only, but to all who have loved his appearing.

**9** Make every effort to come to me soon.
**10** For Demas has abandoned me, because he loved this present age, and has gone to Thessalonica; Crescens has gone to Galatia, Titus to Dalmatia.
**11** Only Luke is with me. Pick up Mark and bring him along, for he is useful to me for service.
**12** I have sent Tychicus to Ephesus.
**13** When you come, bring the cloak that I left with Carpus in Troas, and the books, especially the parchments.
**14** Alexander the metalworker did me much harm – the Lord will repay him according to his works.
**15** You too must be on your guard against him, for he strongly opposed our words.

**16** At my first defence no one stood by me, but all abandoned me – may it not be counted against them!
**17** But the Lord stood by me and gave me strength, so that through me the proclamation might be fully carried out and all the nations might hear it; and I was rescued from the mouth of the lion.
**18** The Lord will rescue me from every evil deed and will save me for his heavenly kingdom. To him be the glory for ever and ever. Amen.

**19** Greet Prisca and Aquila and the household of Onesiphorus.
**20** Erastus stayed in Corinth, and I left Trophimus sick in Miletus.
**21** Make every effort to come before winter. Eubulus greets you, as do Pudens and Linus and Claudia and all the brothers and sisters.

**22** The Lord be with your spirit. Grace be with you all.
