# The Book of Nahum

## Chapter 1

**1** An oracle concerning Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.

> **2** A jealous God, one who takes vengeance, is the LORD;
> the LORD takes vengeance and is full of wrath.
> The LORD takes vengeance on his adversaries
> and holds it against his enemies.
> **3** The LORD is slow to anger and great in power,
> and the LORD will surely not acquit the guilty.
> In whirlwind and in storm is his way,
> and clouds are the dust of his feet.
> **4** He rebukes the sea and makes it dry,
> and all the rivers he dries up.
> Bashan and Carmel wither,
> and the blossom of Lebanon withers.
> **5** The mountains shake because of him,
> and the hills melt away;
> the earth rises up before his face,
> the world and all who live in it.
> **6** Before his fury who can stand?
> Who can rise up against his burning anger?
> His wrath pours out like fire,
> and the rocks are torn down by him.
> **7** The LORD is good,
> a refuge in the day of distress;
> he knows those who shelter in him.
> **8** But with a flood that passes over
> he will make a full end of her place,
> and his enemies he will pursue into darkness.

> **9** What are you devising against the LORD?
> He is making a full end;
> distress will not rise up a second time.
> **10** For even if they are like tangled thorns
> and drunk as with their drink,
> they are devoured like dry stubble, completely.
> **11** From you has come out
> one who devises evil against the LORD,
> one who counsels what is worthless.

> **12** Thus says the LORD:
> Though they are at full strength and ever so many,
> even so they will be shorn and pass away.
> I have afflicted you;
> I will afflict you no more.
> **13** And now I will break his yoke from off you
> and tear your bonds apart.
> **14** But concerning you the LORD has commanded:
> Nothing more will be sown that bears your name.
> From the house of your gods I will cut off
> carved image and cast image;
> I will make your grave,
> for you weigh nothing.

## Chapter 2

> **1** Look, on the mountains
> the feet of one who brings good news,
> who announces peace!
> Keep your festivals, Judah,
> pay your vows!
> For never again will the worthless one
> pass through you;
> he is cut off completely. *(English Bibles: 1:15)*

> **2** One who scatters has come up against you.
> Guard the rampart!
> Watch the road!
> Brace your loins!
> Gather all your strength! *(English Bibles: 2:1; from here on subtract one from the verse number)*
> **3** For the LORD restores the majesty of Jacob
> like the majesty of Israel;
> for plunderers have plundered them
> and ruined their vine branches.
> **4** The shield of his warriors is reddened,
> the men of valour are clothed in scarlet;
> the chariots flash with the fire of steel
> on the day he makes ready,
> and the cypress spears are brandished.
> **5** In the streets the chariots rage,
> they rush to and fro in the squares;
> their appearance is like torches,
> like lightning they dart about.
> **6** He remembers his nobles;
> they stumble as they go;
> they hurry to her wall,
> and the protective cover is set up.
> **7** The gates of the rivers are opened,
> and the palace melts away.
> **8** And it is fixed: she is stripped, she is carried away,
> and her slave women moan
> like the sound of doves,
> beating on their breasts.
> **9** Nineveh is like a pool of water
> all her days;
> but now they are fleeing.
> "Stop! Stop!" –
> but no one turns back.
> **10** Plunder silver! Plunder gold!
> There is no end to the treasure,
> wealth heavy with every precious thing.
> **11** Empty! Emptied! Laid waste!
> The heart melts, the knees knock,
> trembling is in all loins,
> and all their faces grow pale.

> **12** Where now is the lions' den,
> the pasture of the young lions,
> where the lion walked, the lioness,
> there the lion's cub,
> and no one made them afraid?
> **13** The lion tore enough for his cubs
> and strangled prey for his lionesses;
> he filled his holes with prey
> and his dens with torn flesh.
> **14** Look, I am against you
> – declares the LORD of hosts –
> I will burn her chariots in smoke,
> and the sword will devour your young lions;
> I will cut off your prey from the earth,
> and the voice of your messengers
> will be heard no more.

## Chapter 3

> **1** Woe to the city of bloodshed!
> All of it is lies,
> full of plunder;
> the prey never departs.
> **2** The crack of the whip
> and the rumble of the wheel,
> the galloping horse
> and the jolting chariot!
> **3** Horsemen charging,
> the flame of the sword and the lightning of the spear,
> a multitude of slain,
> a heavy mass of corpses,
> no end to the bodies –
> they stumble over their bodies.
> **4** All this because of the many harlotries of the prostitute,
> lovely and charming,
> mistress of sorceries,
> who sells nations through her harlotries
> and clans through her sorceries.
> **5** Look, I am against you
> – declares the LORD of hosts –
> I will lift your skirts over your face
> and let the nations see your nakedness
> and the kingdoms your shame.
> **6** I will throw filth on you,
> treat you with contempt,
> and make you a spectacle.
> **7** And everyone who sees you
> will flee from you and say:
> "Nineveh is devastated!
> Who will grieve for her?"
> Where shall I seek comforters for you?

> **8** Are you better than No-Amon,
> who sat by the channels of the Nile,
> with water all around her,
> whose rampart was the sea,
> her wall from the sea?
> **9** Cush was her strength, and Egypt,
> and there was no end;
> Put and the Libyans
> were among your helpers.
> **10** Yet she too went into exile,
> she went into captivity;
> her little children too were dashed to pieces
> at the head of every street;
> for her honoured men they cast lots,
> and all her great men were bound in chains.
> **11** You too will be drunk,
> you will be hidden away;
> you too will seek a refuge from the enemy.
> **12** All your fortresses are fig trees
> with first-ripe figs:
> if they are shaken,
> they fall into the mouth of the eater.
> **13** Look, your people are women in your midst;
> to your enemies the gates of your land
> are thrown wide open;
> fire has devoured your bars.

> **14** Draw water for the siege!
> Strengthen your fortresses!
> Go into the clay
> and tread the mortar!
> Take hold of the brick mould!
> **15** There the fire will devour you,
> the sword will cut you off;
> it will devour you like the hopper.
> Make yourself many like the hopper,
> make yourself many like the locust!
> **16** You have made your merchants more numerous
> than the stars of the sky;
> the hopper sheds its skin and flies away.
> **17** Your guards are like the locust,
> your officials like a swarm of locusts
> that settle in the walls
> on a cold day;
> when the sun rises, it flees,
> and its place is not known – where are they?

> **18** Your shepherds slumber, king of Assyria;
> your nobles lie down to rest;
> your people are scattered on the mountains,
> and there is no one to assemble them.
> **19** There is no easing for your fracture;
> your wound is grievous.
> All who hear the news of you
> clap their hands over you;
> for over whom has your evil not passed
> continually?
