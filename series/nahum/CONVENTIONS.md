## Nahum specifics
- Verse numbers follow the Hebrew text. Hebrew 2:1 = English 1:15; Hebrew 2:2–14 = English 2:1–13. DE/AF use the Hebrew numbers only; EN gives the Hebrew number and adds the English one in brackets where it differs, e.g. "2:1 (English 1:15)".
- Hannes's docs number by the English chapters (1:9–15, 2:1–7, 2:8–13) and quote the AF 1933/53 text – convert to Hebrew numbering and replace all Nahum quotations with our translation.
- Day 1's doc opens with a welcome and a 6-day study plan table: keep the welcome (shortened), drop the table. Remove every "Studieplan/Vorderingstabel" table and chat line.
- Day 6 closes the series.
