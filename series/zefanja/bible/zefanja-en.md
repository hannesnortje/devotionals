# The Book of Zephaniah

## Chapter 1

**1** The word of the LORD that came to Zephaniah son of Cushi, son of Gedaliah, son of Amariah, son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah.

> **2** I will sweep away, sweep away everything
> from the face of the ground –
> declares the LORD.
> **3** I will sweep away man and beast;
> I will sweep away the birds of the heavens
> and the fish of the sea,
> and the stumbling blocks with the wicked.
> I will cut off mankind
> from the face of the ground –
> declares the LORD.
> **4** I will stretch out my hand against Judah
> and against all the inhabitants of Jerusalem.
> I will cut off from this place the remnant of Baal,
> the name of the idol-priests together with the priests,
> **5** and those who bow down on the rooftops
> to the host of the heavens,
> and those who bow down and swear to the LORD
> and swear by their king,
> **6** and those who turn back from following the LORD,
> who have not sought the LORD
> and have not inquired of him.

> **7** Silence before the Lord, the LORD!
> For the day of the LORD is near;
> for the LORD has prepared a sacrifice,
> he has consecrated those he invited.
> **8** On the day of the LORD's sacrifice
> I will punish the officials and the king's sons
> and all who clothe themselves in foreign clothing.
> **9** And on that day I will punish
> everyone who leaps over the threshold,
> those who fill the house of their master
> with violence and deceit.
> **10** On that day – declares the LORD –
> a cry will sound from the Fish Gate,
> wailing from the Second Quarter,
> and a great crash from the hills.
> **11** Wail, you inhabitants of the Mortar!
> For all the people of Canaan are destroyed;
> all who weigh out silver are cut off.
> **12** At that time I will search Jerusalem with lamps,
> and I will punish the men
> who have thickened on their dregs,
> who say in their heart:
> "The LORD does no good,
> and he does no evil."
> **13** Their wealth will become plunder
> and their houses a desolation.
> They will build houses but not live in them;
> they will plant vineyards but not drink their wine.

> **14** Near is the great day of the LORD,
> near and hurrying very fast.
> Listen! The day of the LORD!
> Bitterly the warrior cries out there.
> **15** A day of rage is that day,
> a day of distress and anguish,
> a day of wreck and ruin,
> a day of darkness and deep gloom,
> a day of cloud and thick darkness,
> **16** a day of ram's horn and battle cry
> against the fortified cities
> and against the lofty corner towers.
> **17** I will bring distress on mankind,
> and they will walk like the blind,
> for they have sinned against the LORD.
> Their blood will be poured out like dust,
> and their flesh like dung.
> **18** Neither their silver nor their gold
> will be able to rescue them
> on the day of the rage of the LORD.
> In the fire of his jealousy
> the whole earth will be devoured;
> for a full end, yes, a terrifying end
> he will make of all the inhabitants of the earth.

## Chapter 2

> **1** Gather yourselves together, yes, gather,
> O nation without longing,
> **2** before the decree is born –
> like chaff the day passes by –
> before there comes upon you
> the burning anger of the LORD,
> before there comes upon you
> the day of the anger of the LORD.
> **3** Seek the LORD, all you humble of the land,
> who have done his justice.
> Seek righteousness, seek humility;
> perhaps you will be hidden
> on the day of the anger of the LORD.

> **4** For Gaza will be forsaken,
> and Ashkelon a desolation;
> Ashdod – at noon they will drive her out,
> and Ekron will be uprooted.
> **5** Woe to the inhabitants of the seacoast,
> the nation of the Cherethites!
> The word of the LORD is against you,
> Canaan, land of the Philistines:
> I will make you perish
> until there is no inhabitant.
> **6** The seacoast will become pastures,
> meadows for shepherds
> and pens for flocks.
> **7** The coast will belong
> to the remnant of the house of Judah;
> there they will graze,
> in the houses of Ashkelon
> they will lie down at evening.
> For the LORD their God will care for them
> and turn back their captivity.

> **8** I have heard the reproach of Moab
> and the revilings of the Ammonites,
> with which they reproached my people
> and made themselves great against their territory.
> **9** Therefore, as I live –
> declares the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel –
> Moab will become like Sodom,
> and the Ammonites like Gomorrah:
> a place possessed by nettles, and a salt pit,
> and a desolation for ever.
> The remnant of my people will plunder them,
> and the rest of my nation will inherit them.
> **10** This they will have for their pride,
> because they reproached and made themselves great
> against the people of the LORD of hosts.
> **11** Fearsome will the LORD be against them,
> for he will make all the gods of the earth waste away;
> and they will bow down to him, each from its place,
> all the coastlands of the nations.

> **12** You too, Cushites –
> they are slain by my sword.
> **13** And he will stretch out his hand against the north
> and make Assyria perish,
> and he will make Nineveh a desolation,
> dry as the wilderness.
> **14** Herds will lie down in her midst,
> every beast of a nation;
> the desert owl and the hedgehog
> will lodge among her capitals.
> A voice will sing in the window,
> devastation on the doorstep,
> for the cedar work is laid bare.
> **15** This is the exulting city
> that sat secure,
> that said in her heart:
> "I am, and there is none besides me."
> How she has become a desolation,
> a lair for wild beasts!
> Everyone who passes by her
> hisses and shakes his fist.

## Chapter 3

> **1** Woe to the rebellious and defiled one,
> the oppressing city!
> **2** She has not listened to the voice;
> she has not accepted discipline.
> In the LORD she has not trusted;
> to her God she has not drawn near.
> **3** Her officials in her midst
> are roaring lions;
> her judges are wolves at evening
> that leave nothing for the morning.
> **4** Her prophets are reckless,
> men of treachery;
> her priests profane what is holy;
> they do violence to the instruction.
> **5** The LORD in her midst is righteous;
> he does no injustice.
> Morning by morning he brings his justice to light;
> it never fails.
> But the unjust knows no shame.

> **6** I have cut off nations;
> their corner towers are desolate.
> I have laid waste their streets,
> with no one passing through.
> Their cities lie in ruins,
> with no one left, with no inhabitant.
> **7** I said: "Surely you will fear me,
> you will accept discipline" –
> then her dwelling would not be cut off,
> all that I had appointed for her.
> But they rose early
> and made all their deeds corrupt.
> **8** Therefore wait for me – declares the LORD –
> for the day when I rise up for plunder.
> For my judgment is to gather nations,
> to assemble kingdoms,
> to pour out on them my fury,
> all my burning anger;
> for in the fire of my jealousy
> the whole earth will be devoured.

> **9** For then I will change the lip of the peoples
> into a pure lip,
> so that all of them call on the name of the LORD
> and serve him shoulder to shoulder.
> **10** From beyond the rivers of Cush
> my worshippers, the daughter of my dispersed ones,
> will bring my offering.
> **11** On that day you will not be put to shame
> for all your deeds
> by which you rebelled against me;
> for then I will remove from your midst
> those who exult in your pride,
> and you will no longer be haughty
> on my holy mountain.
> **12** I will leave in your midst
> a people poor and lowly,
> and they will shelter in the name of the LORD.
> **13** The remnant of Israel
> will do no injustice and speak no lie,
> and no deceitful tongue
> will be found in their mouth.
> For they will graze and lie down,
> and no one will make them afraid.

> **14** Sing for joy, Daughter Zion!
> Shout aloud, Israel!
> Be glad and exult with all your heart,
> Daughter Jerusalem!
> **15** The LORD has taken away the judgments against you;
> he has turned away your enemy.
> The King of Israel, the LORD, is in your midst;
> you will fear evil no more.
> **16** On that day it will be said to Jerusalem:
> "Do not fear, Zion!
> Do not let your hands hang limp!
> **17** The LORD your God is in your midst,
> a warrior who saves.
> He beams with gladness over you;
> he is silent in his love;
> he rejoices over you with a song of joy."

> **18** Those who grieve far from the appointed feast I have gathered;
> they were from you –
> a burden on her, a reproach.
> **19** Look, at that time I will deal
> with all who afflict you.
> I will save the lame
> and assemble the outcast,
> and I will make them a praise and a name
> in all the earth where they were put to shame.
> **20** At that time I will bring you home,
> at the time when I assemble you;
> for I will make you a name and a praise
> among all the peoples of the earth,
> when I turn back your captivity
> before your eyes, says the LORD.
